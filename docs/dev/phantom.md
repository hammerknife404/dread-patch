# The dread phantom

Part of the notes for agents working here: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) has the rules and the index.

## The dread phantom (`entity/DreadPhantom`, `entity/PhantomCalling`, `magic/PhantomRules`, `effect/RimefrostEffect`, `spell/SummonDreadPhantomsSpell`, `client/DreadPhantomRenderer`, `client/DreadPhantomSkin(s)`; 0.45.0)

- **The entity:** `dreadpatch:dread_phantom`, a vanilla `Phantom` subclass (its box, tracking, attributes plus follow range 64, size 0, so 20 health and a 6 bite) and an `IDreadMob` (`getCommander` is its summoner; `isAlliedTo` and `canAttack` keep it off the Dread; it refuses Withering Rime, Whiteout and Rimefrost). `isSunBurnTick` false. Tagged `#minecraft:undead` and freeze-immune.
- **Its flight is copied, not reused.** Vanilla's goals and `PhantomMoveControl` share package-private fields (`moveTargetPoint`, `anchorPoint`, `attackPhase`), so `DreadPhantom` carries its own copies of the move control, strategy, dive and circle goals on fields of ours (`moveTarget`, `anchor`, `diving`), saved under vanilla's `AX`/`AY`/`AZ` (written after vanilla's, so ours win), plus `Summoner`. Changes from vanilla's:
  - any living target, not only players;
  - **vanilla's dive drops its target after every bite** (`PhantomSweepAttackGoal.stop` → `setTarget(null)`), relying on its player scan to pick one again; ours keeps it, or revenge would last one dive;
  - **a dive is committed (0.45.1):** vanilla's pulls out when the phantom is hurt (`hurtTime > 0`); ours goes on through hits until it bites or meets a wall (the owner, after trying 0.45.0: "phantoms work, make them commit to hitting and ignore being hit causing them to retreat");
  - time between dives by difficulty (`PhantomRules.swoopTicks`: vanilla's 8–11 s on Easy, 6–8 Normal, 4–6 Hard), and a fresh target (any `setTarget` to a new one) is dived on within `firstSwoopTicks` (10/5/2 ticks);
  - idle, its anchor is 10 over its summoner (within 96 blocks), so it escorts them; it takes its summoner's target every second while it has none.
- **Targets:** `RevengeGoal` (its own: vanilla's `HurtByTargetGoal` and Iron's `GenericCopyOwnerTargetGoal` need a `PathfinderMob`; it also sets its summoner's other phantoms within 32 on the attacker), `AvengeSummonerGoal`, and vanilla's player hunt (only while it has no target). **Follow range 64:** a `TargetGoal` drops a target past follow range, and a phantom circles 20–40 blocks over its target.
- **The bite** (`doHurtTarget`): Rimefrost (100 ticks) at 35% and Withering Rime (60 ticks) at 15%, rolled apart. Its rime feeds no one.
- **Calling** (`PhantomCalling`, on `EntityTickEvent.Post`): a `DreadQueen`, any Ice and Fire `DreadLichEntity`, and a `Syndrigossa` that isn't tame or a corpse, while alive, not NoAI, with a live target. A countdown in the summoner's persistent data (`dreadpatch:phantom_call`, ticking only while it has a target): first 300, then 900; 1–2 a call, never past 4 of its own within 128 (a full roster spends the call); no room, it tries again in 20. They rise 1–4 blocks over the summoner's head, up to 3 to the side, through `EventHooks.finalizeMobSpawn` (`MOB_SUMMONED`), set on its target, and despawn like monsters.
- **The spell** (scroll only, in her loot list, not on her staff): Rimefrost (Ice until 0.51.0), epic, one level, 50 mana, 90 s cooldown. `SummonedCourt.raisePhantoms`: 2, marked with `dreadpatch:court_expires` (60 s) and kept till then, so they're court members (`SummonedCourt.isMember` takes a `DreadPhantom` too; `commanderId` reads its `Summoner`; `dismiss` takes a kind, so each spell's recast dismisses only its own). On joining a level, a member phantom's targets are Iron's owner goals plus its `RevengeGoal`, without the follow goal (it escorts in the air). A member drops nothing (`shouldDropLoot`) and gives no XP.
- **Drops** (`entities/dread_phantom`): Ice and Fire's thrall table nested (a `minecraft:loot_table` entry), then under `killed_by_player` 1–3 phantom membrane and 1–3 Rimefrost dragon scales, each +0–1 a Looting level.
- **Rimefrost** (`effect/RimefrostEffect`): −25% movement (`ADD_MULTIPLIED_TOTAL`), harmful, pale rime blue; its icon from `make_icons.py`.
- **Sounds:** all at 45% (0.64.0, the owner: "Lower all dread phantom sounds by 55% globally": `getSoundVolume` × `PhantomRules.VOLUME`, which `makeSound` and its ghostlier layer use, and its swoops' own volumes ×0.45 too). `makeSound` (ambient, hurt and death all pass through it) plays vanilla's phantom sound and then ours; the dive plays `PHANTOM_SWOOP` and ours. Ours (`assets/dreadpatch/sounds.json`, registered in `ModRegistries`) point at vanilla's own files: ghast moans pitched up and vex idles pitched down (wail), vex hurts (hurt), ghast and vex deaths (death), vex charges and soul sand valley whispers (swoop). No subtitles: vanilla's phantom ones already show.
- **Its look** (`DreadPhantomSkin`, game-free, tested; `DreadPhantomSkins` caches it, cleared on a resource reload): vanilla's `phantom.png` and `phantom_eyes.png`, read as they load:
  - blue hide → soot/slate keeping 30% of its blue, pink flesh → withered grey, beige bone → the Dread's pale bone, green eyes → cyan (the eyes layer is ours, `RenderType.eyes` of the made texture, in place of vanilla's);
  - its wings: a bone along the leading edge and four fingers fanning from the wrist (where the tip meets the base), and value-noise holes through the membrane, more toward the trailing edge and the tip, fewer by a bone or the body;
  - **vanilla's box UV:** the region at `(u + depth, v)` is the box's `DOWN` face, drawn upward on an entity; `(u + depth + width, v)` its `UP` face, the underside. Both run u along +x and their top row is the box's max z (the wing's trailing edge). So a hole cut at the same spot in both is see-through, and the mirrored right wing (u from its root too) tears the same. The trailing-edge strip (`SOUTH`) runs against x.
  - Any whole multiple of 64 is torn; another size is only recoloured.
- **Its trail:** client-side, every 3 ticks at 60%, off its tail: our `rime_flame_small` (the breath's cyan flame at the bolt's size; since 0.50.0, the owner's) 15% of the time, else a snowflake or our `rime_smoke_small`. Vanilla's mycelium motes stay.
- **Natural spawning:** in the Dreadlands only, since 0.46.0 (`Dreadlands.onLevelTick`, [dreadlands.md](dreadlands.md)), more often round spires since 0.52.0.

## Risen Dread (`entity/MiniatureDreadPhantom`, `entity/RisenDread`, `client/RisenDreadClient`, `world/RisenSpawns`, `magic/RisenRules`; 0.63.0)

- **The owner** (the dump's miniature phantoms from bats and parrots, and "HP-Scaled Dread Water Beasts"; asked what turns them: "Dying to whiteout dominion or other conditions that would turn a corpse into a dread mob"; the water beasts "intended for overworld only or places that have water and aquatic life"; the miniature phantoms "Same as the Dread water beasts basically"; then "allow the smaller dread phantoms rarely spawn in caves, same as the aquatic beast variant in the rare chance of underground water").
- **Rising:** `WhiteoutDominion.raise` (her storm's, the only raising of the dead so far) asks `RisenDread.rise` first: `#dreadpatch:rise_as_miniature_phantoms` (bat, parrot) gives a miniature phantom (`MOB_SUMMONED`, her its summoner); `#minecraft:aquatic` or any `WaterAnimal` gives a Rime Eel (0.71.0, below; `MOB_SUMMONED`, `risen_by` her; from 0.63.0 to 0.70.0 a new one of its own type loaded from its tag, made Dread). Either takes her target. `canRise` refuses anything already Dread (`RisenDread.dread`: `IDreadMob` or risen).
- **The miniature phantom** (`dreadpatch:miniature_dread_phantom`): a `DreadPhantom` subclass, the same box and `generic.scale` 0.5 (vanilla's `LivingEntity` halves its box and its renderer the drawing); 6 health, a 2 bite (its `setPhantomSize` sets it back after vanilla's `updatePhantomSizeInfo`, which sets 6 + size), voice ×1.5, 2 XP; `DreadPhantomRenderer`; undead and freeze-immune; its table 0–1 phantom membrane on a player's kill (+0–1 a Looting level); spawn egg in Spawn Eggs. Caps that count `DreadPhantom.class` (her calling's 4, the Dreadlands' 4) count it too.
- **Made Dread** (`RisenDread.makeDread`): persistent `dreadpatch:risen` (and `risen_by`, its raiser), permanent modifiers `risen_health` (×2, `ADD_MULTIPLIED_BASE` +1) and `risen_speed` (×1.6), healed whole. On every `EntityJoinLevelEvent` (goals aren't saved): panic and avoid goals out; `BiteGoal` (1: paths at its target, every 20 ticks within twice its width plus the target's and in sight, `mobAttack` for `RisenRules.bite` = 1 + 15% of its kind's base health, then the phantom's two chances), `RaiserTargetGoal` (its raiser's target, a second at a time, while it has none), `HurtByTargetGoal` (a `PathfinderMob`), players. Its own bite: a fish has no `attack_damage`, and `Mob.doHurtTarget` would throw on reading it.
- **Of the Dread, to us** (`RisenDread.dread`): refuses Withering Rime, Whiteout and Rimefrost (`DreadEvents`), and `RimefireCrown.canHarm` spares it from a Dread caster's magic and storm (or it'd die again in hers). Ice and Fire's dread mobs still prey on it (their tests know `IDreadMob` only).
- **Drawn** (`RisenDreadClient`): `Marked(entity id)` (payload) on `PlayerEvent.StartTracking`; on a `RenderLivingEvent.Pre` for a marked one, the draw is cancelled and done again through a buffer source whose consumers multiply every vertex's colour by 0.55, 0.70, 0.88. A mob drawn by anything but a `LivingEntityRenderer` (GeckoLib) shows untinted.
- **Stirring on their own** (`RisenSpawns`, the overworld and the Dreadlands, every 200 ticks a player): one in `cave_dread_phantoms` (40) a miniature phantom in dark air (block light 7 or less, two clear, no sky, 4+ under the top, 12–32 off, within 12 up or down), one in `cave_dread_water` (40) a Dread glow squid 3 : squid 1 : cod 1 : salmon 1 in underground water (a source with water or air over it, likewise placed), `NATURAL`, no raiser; never past 3 of either within 64. In the Dreadlands (0.71.0) that water roll gives a Rime Eel instead (below).

## The Rime Eel (`entity/RimeEel`, `client/RimeEelModel`, `client/RimeEelRenderer`, `magic/RimeEelRules`, `world/RisenSpawns`; 0.71.0)

- **The owner:** a pasted spec and a picture (2026-11-25): "an elongated, serpentine dread-anglerfish mob native to the Dreadlands, scaling in size from a standard fish up to a squid (~0.6 to 1.4 blocks long) with HP scaling roughly from 10–18 and 4 natural armor points"; spawning "in dark water sources (minimum 1x2 water blocks) at light level 6 or below"; "any aquatic-type creature slain during the Dread Queen's Whiteout Dominion becomes a Rime Eel"; eats "dropped food items—including rotten flesh, ink sacs, and most edible items—healing roughly 0.25 to 1 HP per item"; drops "1–2 bones, Dread Shards, rarely Rotten Flesh, and has a 5% chance to drop 1–2 Rime Dragon Scales"; its lure "flickers erratically—remaining off far more often than on—while emitting a short-range cryo-entropic aura pulse every few seconds that inflicts either Rimefrost or Whiteout Vision for a few seconds. Anything caught and affected by the aura's light immediately triggers a Frenzy for a few seconds, doubling the eel's attack speed, while each melee bite carries a low chance to inflict Withering Rime"; "swims swiftly, easily scales up single blocks, and takes no drowning or suffocation damage when out of water; on land, it slithers like a serpent—reserving its forward lunge strictly for combat or pursuing targets, with a cooldown between lunges—and its terrestrial AI gives equal priority to seeking nearby water sources and pursuing nearby non-Dread mobs". Asked, the storm's water creatures "always a Rime Eel", and the Dreadlands' water "eels only" (the overworld's cave water kept as it was). Where the spec gave no number, the number is ours (`RimeEelRules`).
- **The entity** (`dreadpatch:rime_eel`, `MONSTER`): a `Monster` and `IDreadMob` (its commander its raiser: `RisenDread.raiser`), allied to `IDreadMob`, never attacking the Dread. Box 1.6 × 1.0 at scale 1; `generic.scale` rolled in `finalizeSpawn`: 0.3-1.25 (0.6-2.5 across; the curled model's 2 at scale 1), or one in 20 an alpha or matron at 1.25-2 (2.5-4 across; `RimeEelRules.alpha`, by size only for now); health 10-18 and bite 2-4 by size to 2.5 across, and on up the same line for an alpha (to 24.5 and 5.5), to the half. A summon with NBT skips `finalizeSpawn`, so the attribute defaults are a smaller one's (`scale(0.5)`, 1.55 across, 14, 3). 4 armour, speed 1.0, step 1, 5 XP. Undead, aquatic, freeze-immune, `can_breathe_under_water` (vanilla's check is final); immune to `drown` and `in_wall`. Its loot table: 1-2 bones and 1-2 dread shards (+0-1 a Looting level each), rotten flesh at 10% (+Looting), 1-2 Rimefrost Dragon Scales at 5% (+Looting), no player needed.
- **Moving:** as an axolotl: `AmphibiousPathNavigation`, `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl` (85, 10; ×0.035 in water, ×0.2 on land; buoyant), `SmoothSwimmingLookControl`, its own `travel` in water (push, then ×0.9 drag), water malus 0, not pushed by fluid.
- **Goals:** `BiteGoal` (a `MeleeAttackGoal`, 2: every 20 ticks, 10 in a Frenzy) and `SeekWaterGoal` (2: out of water, the nearest water within 12 across and 4 up or down) share a priority and the move flag, so whichever starts first keeps it ("equal priority"); `EatFoodGoal` (3: no target; the nearest item within 8 it `eats`, not on pickup delay; in reach (within half its width + 1 across, 1.5 under to its height + 1.5 over) one item every 10 ticks); random swimming (5), strolling on land only (6), looking. Targets: hurt-by (1), its raiser's target (`RisenDread.RaiserTargetGoal`, 2), the nearest player or mob not of the Dread, not a dragon, boss or armour stand (3). Its goals' "now and then" checks are a 1-in-5 roll, not `tickCount % 10`: vanilla runs a goal's `canUse` only on alternate ticks by `server tick + id`, so a tick-count step missed every check for some eels (the smoke test's eat check failed one run in two).
- **Food** (`RimeEel.eats`): `#dreadpatch:rime_eel_eats` (ink sacs, glow ink sacs) or anything with `minecraft:food` not in `#dreadpatch:rime_eel_refuses` (golden apples, enchanted golden apples, golden carrots). It heals nutrition / 8, 0.25 to 1.
- **In a fight** (`aiStep`, its target alive and prey): every 60-100 ticks a pulse (`pulse`): Rimefrost II or Whiteout (a coin each) for 80 ticks on each living thing within 4 not of the Dread (creative and spectators aside); if any took it, a Frenzy of 80 ticks (saved as `Frenzy`). And (0.75.1, the owner: "should also apply glowing to other creatures, and not to itself or other rimefrost eels") vanilla's Glowing for 80 ticks on every living thing within 4 but Rime Eels, the Dread too (creative and spectators aside); it doesn't count toward the Frenzy by itself, but (0.76.0, the owner: "Highlighted creatures also send rime eels into frenzies") any eel whose target is Glowing, from anything, keeps a Frenzy of at least `GLOW_FRENZY_TICKS` (20) while it glows. Only an eel with its lantern pulses (0.76.0, asked). Entity event 101 to clients (the lure's flare), a ring of snowflakes and a few glow motes, its pulse sound. A bite (`doHurtTarget`) has a 10% chance of 60 ticks of Withering Rime, laid by it. On land, on the ground, in sight, 1.5-5 off and its 40-tick cooldown run: a lunge (0.7 toward, 0.3 up).
- **Drawn** (0.71.1, the owner, at 0.71.0's small angler: "I was expecting it to look like this picture", the spec's picture again; and of the angler: "keep that angler model for later though i like it", so it's on the branch `angler-model`, at `ce5c981`). `tools/models/make_rime_eel.py` holds the model once and writes from it the geometry (`client/RimeEelGeometry`, generated), its 128 × 64 skin, eyes and lure, and with `--preview DIR` renders of it posed at rest (each texel a quad, painter-sorted, as vanilla's `ModelPart.Cube` lays the UVs), to set against the picture without a client. After the picture: a boxy skull 8 × 6 × 9 with a jagged crest, its jaw hanging open (0.6) on fangs (cut out every other pixel), a dark mouth behind, a black eye each side with a cyan pupil; the lure a stalk up from the brow, an arm arching 9 forward over the snout, a hook and an iron-capped lantern; fanned, spined fins; seven ribbed segments (each a raised ring at its front and sawtooth spines on the first five) curling round behind (turned 0.35-0.5 each, the first dipping into the water), to a tail fin. The head is set off to one side and back, so the curled eel's middle is the entity's. Curled it's 2.06 × 1.88 across and 1.45 tall at scale 1 (`MODEL_LENGTH` 2; box 1.6 × 1.0). `RimeEelModel` resets every part to its pose, takes all but a tenth of the body's curl away as it moves (`limbSwingAmount` × 6; 0.71.2, the owner: "its tail is stuck in that pose"), drawing the head forward and over (to 0, −12) so the straight eel stays on its middle, and adds its wave (in water slight and slow, on land wider), a head bob, the jaw's breathing and bite, the lure's sway, the lantern's swing and the fins' beat. Glow layers (`RenderType.eyes`): its pupils always, its lantern only while `lureLit` (`RimeEelRules.lureLit`: hashed from its UUID and 3-tick steps, lit about a quarter of ticks; and 10 ticks after a pulse); while lit, glow motes and snowflakes round the lantern (`RimeEel.lantern`, its model position turned and scaled as vanilla draws it), where its pulse's motes rise too.
- **Drawn again** (0.76.0, the owner: "The Rime Eels should look exaclty like they do in the photo", a new picture; then, at its renders, "The small lantern on the rime eel variant needs to stick out more to not clip with its own head, and it has slight gravity on the lantern. It retracts closwer when idle and extends out when searching. The eyes still need the cyan dread touch"). The script's model rebuilt, its sheet 128 × 128: bone with a cold cyan cast (`LIGHT` … `DARK`); a skull 9 × 6 × 10 with long fangs hanging from its lip and a jaw open 0.42 on teeth, a black eye each side with a pale glint over a cyan pupil (both glowing); no crest, but a jagged dorsal fin on the first segment; pectoral fins of bare bony rays (their webbing cut out past the root); eight ribbed segments (`SEGMENTS`: 8 to 3 wide, turning 0.15 then up to 0.75 each, the first dipping 0.2 and the second levelling out), lying flat and curling round into a ring behind the head, to the tail fin. The lure: a stalk 12 up from the top of the head leaning back, an arm 15 reaching forward and down past the snout, a hook, and a vanilla lantern's shape (a nub, a cap, glass 4 × 4 × 4, a base), clear of the head. Curled 2.14 × 1.96 across, 1.57 tall at scale 1 (`MODEL_LENGTH` kept at 2); straight, its head drawn to (0, −18).
  - **Its lantern** (`RimeEel.LANTERN`, synced, saved as `Lantern`; an eel saved before had one): one spawn in `LANTERN_ONE_IN` (6, the owner: "Only about 10-25% spawn with the lantern"). Without it, the stalk, arm, hook and lantern aren't drawn (the owner: "no fish horns"), the lure never lights and it never pulses.
  - **Reach** (`RimeEel.reach`, on both sides): toward 1 by `REACH_STEP` (0.08) a tick while it's aggressive or moving, toward 0 otherwise. Drawn in, the model adds `RETRACT_STALK` (−0.6) to the stalk's x turn and `RETRACT_ARM` (−0.1) to the arm's (the script's `RETRACT`, which renders it as `rime_eel_lure_in.png`), so the lantern sits just over the head. `RimeEel.lantern` (its sparkles, its pulse's motes) lerps between `RimeEelRules.LANTERN_IN` and `LANTERN_OUT`, which the script prints.
  - **Hanging plumb:** the lantern's x turn is set to undo its parents' (head, stalk, arm, hook) every frame, and its z the stalk's sway, so it hangs straight down; then a slight swing, and up to 0.35 trailing back as it moves.
- **Sounds** (vanilla's, `sounds.json`): a guardian's ambient (in water) or flop (ashore), hurt (with a skeleton's, softer) and death, deeper; its pulse a conduit's strike over amethyst; its bite an axolotl's, its lunge a dolphin's; smaller ones higher (an alpha's lowest).
- **Spawning** (`RisenSpawns`, the Dreadlands, every 200 ticks a player): the `cave_dread_water` roll (40) takes the dark underground water it finds; `chasma_dread_water` (8) a pocket; `dreadlands_rime_eels` (10) any dark water: 24 columns 8-32 off, from 16 over the player (or the surface) to 16 under. Each spot a source with water over it (1 × 2) and `RimeEelRules.darkEnough` (block light, or the sky's capped at the Dreadlands' sun of 6: so any water there short of a torch). `NATURAL`, never past 4 eels within 64; the risen cap doesn't count them.
