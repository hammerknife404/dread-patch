# The Dread Gate and the Dreadlands

Part of the notes for agents working here: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) has the rules and the index.

## The Dread Gate and the key

**The shape** (`magic/GateShape`, after vanilla's `PortalShape`)
- An open rectangle, 2–21 wide and 3–21 tall (vanilla's Nether portal's, since 0.55.1; 2–5 by 3–7 before), with frame along all four sides. The corners are optional.
- It's found from any open cell inside.
- **Frame:** `#dreadpatch:dread_gate_frame`, which is Ice and Fire's `dread_stone`, `_bricks`, `_bricks_chiseled`, `_bricks_cracked`, `_bricks_mossy`, `_tile` and `dread_stone_face`.
- **Open:** air.

**The Mausoleum's ready-made gates**
- The far hall's ice forge shrine has four arches, each 3 wide and 6 tall inside, framed entirely in the tag's blocks.
- So every Mausoleum, old worlds' included, has four gates, and nothing of Ice and Fire's structure is changed or shipped.

**Opening it** (`entity/DreadGate.onUseKey`, on `PlayerInteractEvent.RightClickBlock`, both sides)
- **Trigger:** a `dread_key` used on a frame block, with air at the clicked face.
- **The plane:** a side face gives its axis; a top or bottom face tries X, then Z.
- **No gate there:** an action-bar hint.
- **A gate:** the event is cancelled with success.
- **On Peaceful:** it opens as anywhere (it brings no one since 0.70.0; until 0.46.0 the gate refused there and kept the key).
- **On the server:**
  - a `DreadGate` entity is made, holding the axis;
  - it fills the opening;
  - the key is used up (`ItemStack.consume`; kept in creative).

**Open** (`DreadGate`; 0.70.0, the owner: "Leaving a dread portal open no longer summons the Dread Queen or Syndragossa. It remains just a portal and does not despawn if players go in. It should slowly build a few layers of snow layers around it up to 3-4 blocks out")
- **Saving:** the entity is saved. Summoned bare, it finds its gate round itself on either axis, or discards itself. Saves from before 0.70.0 kept `Side` and `Activator`, now ignored; a keyed gate saved mid-countdown (`OpenFor` ≥ 0) still runs it out.
- **Panes:**
  - set with `UPDATE_CLIENTS | UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE`, as vanilla's `PortalShape` fills (otherwise the first pane sees open air and falls);
  - every tick it checks every pane is still there (faded out, that its frame still stands whole round the opening), or it shatters.
- **Open till its frame breaks:** a keyed gate's `OpenFor` stays −1. Its cold fire and snow pour from its faces, 2 to 4 particles every 2 ticks, reaching their fullest over `RITUAL_TICKS` (200); the portal's ambient sound every 2 s.
- **Snow** (`laySnow`): every `GateTiming.SNOW_TICKS` (40) one try at a random column within `SNOW_REACH` (4) of its opening's foot (a box the opening's width + 4 each way along it, 4 each way across; not the gate's own plane and frame), its distance from the foot's nearest block giving the most layers there (`GateTiming.snowLayers`: 3 to 1.5, 2 to 2.5, 1 to 4). Down from 2 over the foot, 6 blocks: snow already lying gets a layer if under its most; air over a block snow survives on gets one; anything else, nothing. So it never replaces a block, and it's slow: a ring round a 3-wide gate is about a hundred columns, some minutes to fill.
- **Until 0.70.0, the ritual:** at `RITUAL_TICKS` (200; 100 until 0.46.0) it summoned a `DreadQueen` as the boss (`MobSpawnType.EVENT`), on an ancient Syndrigossa where a probe one fitted in front of it, else on foot at its foot, her target the opener, and shattered; unless someone stepped in, which held it open `openTicks` (300–600) more, fading at the end.
- **Stepping in (0.46.0):** every tick its panes are up, a player (alive, not a spectator) in the slab of its opening ±0.3 round its plane goes through (`passThrough`), with the portal's trigger sound. Saved: `Homeward`, `OpenFor`, `Shown`, `Arrival*`.
  - Into the Dreadlands: the arrival is found for the first through (`Dreadlands.arrival`) and kept for the rest, so a party lands together. Where each came from is the gate's front on the side they stepped in from (their previous tick's position), facing away (`cameFrom`), saved in `DreadlandsData`.
  - A gate in the Dreadlands is **homeward**: each through goes to where they came in (`Dreadlands.goHome`).
- **The Dread coming out** (`DreadGateBlock.randomTick`, `GateTiming.spawnsDread`; 0.76.0, the owner: "random dread mob spawns from an active dreadlands portal, about double the chance nether portals do for pigmen"): as vanilla's `NetherPortalBlock` brings a zombified piglin, on each pane's random tick (a pane whose ritual is gone is cleared instead), out of the Dreadlands, with `doMobSpawning`: a roll under `SPAWN_ROLL` (1,000; vanilla's 2,000) below the difficulty's id (so never on Peaceful). Down through the panes to the frame; on its sturdy top, a random one of her court by its weights (`DreadPatrols.loose`: thrall, ghoul, knight, scuttler), facing out of either face, if it has room; `finalizeMobSpawn`, `NATURAL` (it despawns as the Dread do), its mount (none so far) sent away.
- **Its particles** (0.76.0, the owner: "the dread portal particles should not use the dragon scaled variant, but the smaller player sized kind"): `rime_flame_small` (the Withering Rime-Bolt's) where its faces pour and its blocks shatter, not `rime_flame` (Syndrigossa's breath's); the panes' own `animateTick` used the small one already.
- **The gate home at her fall** (`openReturn`, below) is the only one that still closes on its own: `OpenFor` its `RETURN_TICKS`; over its last `FADE_TICKS` (100) its panes go and come back (`GateTiming.shown`: blinks from 16 ticks, each 0.86 of the last, down to 2), set as its filling; then it shatters, its frame left.

**The pane** (`block/DreadGateBlock`)
- **Properties:** no item, no loot, no collision, light 11, unbreakable except in creative.
- **Model:** `HORIZONTAL_AXIS`, with the Nether portal's models and our texture.
- **Collapse:** `updateShape` turns it to air when a neighbour in its plane is neither pane nor frame, so a broken frame collapses it.
- **Cleanup:** a random tick with no `DreadGate` within 8 blocks clears it.

**The key's recipe** (`data/dreadpatch/recipe/dread_key.json`)
- `SBS / S / GG`: dread shards, a bone, `#c:ingots/gold`.
- Unlocked by holding a dread shard.
- The tooltip lines on Ice and Fire's key come from `DreadPatchClient`.

## The Dreadlands (`world/Dreadlands`, `world/DreadlandsData`, `world/SpireStructure`, `world/SpirePiece`, `magic/SpireShape`, `magic/GateTiming`; 0.46.0)

- **The dimension** is data: `dimension/dreadlands.json` (a noise generator, a fixed biome source), `dimension_type/dreadlands.json` (the overworld's sky and height, `natural` false, `bed_works` false so beds explode, no anchors, no raids), `worldgen/noise_settings/dreadlands.json`, two noises, `worldgen/biome/dreadlands.json`. A mod's dimension JSON appears in existing worlds too.
- **Time and weather are the overworld's for free:** every level but the overworld reads its data through `DerivedLevelData`, so its day and its rain (snow there) follow. **Lightning won't strike there on its own:** `ServerLevel.tickChunk` only strikes where `isRainingAt`, and in a biome that cold that's snow, never rain. The storms' lightning (0.48.0) has to be ours.
- **Terrain** (since 0.66.0: `tools/datagen/make_dreadlands_terrain.py` writes it, with `world/DreadTerrain` and `magic/TerrainRules`; below, The terrain): before 0.66.0, one biome, no caves, aquifers or ore veins, ground at 72 ± about 14 with dune fields, sea level 64 filling the hollows with frozen lakes. no caves, aquifers or ore veins; `final_density` is `y_clamped_gradient` (zero at y 72, a block of height per 1/24) plus 0.42 × a 2D rolling noise (`dreadlands_rolling`, octave −9), the drifts and 0.06 × hummocks (`dreadlands_hummocks`, octave −6), interpolated: ground at 72 ± about 14. **The drifts (0.53.0**, the owner's "rolling hills like wind-swept snowdrifts and dunes"; 0.17 × hummocks before): 0.32 × fields × (0.55 − 1.1 × |`dreadlands_drifts`|) (octave −6, amplitudes 1, 0.4), a ridged noise: long sinuous crests about 4 blocks over the swell where the noise crosses zero, troughs as far under it, so about 8 blocks crest to trough, some 30–60 apart (0.16 looked flat in a heightmap of the ground: 6 blocks over 192). **Only in dune fields** (the owner: "allow for flatter areas too, the dunes are an occasional thing"): fields = clamp(4 × (`dreadlands_dune_fields` − 0.15), 0, 1) (octave −9, one amplitude), blobs a few hundred blocks across over about a third of the waste; elsewhere it's the swell and faint hummocks. Checked in heightmaps sampled through `/tp` feedback (`execute positioned over motion_blocking`, chunks forceloaded a tile of 256 at a time: it reads the floor in an unloaded chunk). Chunks generated before keep their ground, so a seam of a few blocks can show where old meets new. Sea level 64, so the deepest hollows hold water, and the biome's `freeze_top_layer` freezes them and lays snow on every top block (and on the spires). Surface: gravel under water; else packed ice, blue ice or Ice and Fire's dreadstone in noise bands, otherwise snow blocks, with packed ice or snow below to the surface depth, then stone. Bedrock floor.
- **The biome:** temperature −0.7 (snow), grey sky (`0x5E6878`) and fog, drifting snowflakes, vanilla's cave mood (its loop and additions are ours since 0.54.0, below), its music ours (0.60.0, below), no spawners, no carvers. Lang has `biome.`/`dimension.dreadpatch.dreadlands` for mods that show them.
- **Spires** (`SpireStructure`, `dreadpatch:spire`, in `#dreadpatch:spires`): `structure_set/spires.json` random spread 34 / 14 chunks (one in about 540 blocks); `has_structure/spire` is only the Dreadlands. It stands at its chunk's middle, on the lowest of the ground under its foot (`getBaseHeight` at the middle and 8 points round its base radius). One `SpirePiece` (saved as its base and seed) draws its `SpireShape`:
  - 64–100 tall, base radius 11–14 narrowing (concave) to 6–8, then over its last `FLARE_HEIGHT` (8) flaring out (smoothstep) by 3–4 to a flat summit of 9–12 (`summitRadius`; 0.53.0, the owner: "slightly wider to accommodate Syndragossa's size"), faces roughened by value noise (2 blocks at the foot, 0.5 at the top); a foundation 12 deep; `SpireShapeTest` checks it narrows to the flare and widens over it;
  - a ledge winding up outside, a turn every 22 blocks, 2 thick, a block into the face out to 2.6 beyond it, 3 clear over it (cut through the body and the ground); `SpireShapeTest` walks it round and checks no step is over a block;
  - 8 ice spikes (3–6 tall) round the summit's rim, none where the ledge comes up;
  - stone, dreadstone and cracked bricks at the foot, dreadstone and its bricks through the middle with packed and blue ice strata, packed ice, snow and blue ice in the top 18%. The ledge is dreadstone bricks. Ice and Fire's dreadstone placed by its default state is breakable (its `unbreakable` property is false).
  - **No plain ice since 0.47.0** (0.46.0 had it in the top): a torch near it (the encounter's, a player's) melts it to water running down the spire. Spires already half-generated in a world keep what they have.
  - **An encounter spire** (`SpireShape.encounter`, 50% by seed): a 3×3 dais on the summit's middle (dreadstone tiles round chiseled bricks) and an Ice and Fire dread torch at ±3, ±3 (`SpireShape.summit`).
  - **Its summit chest (0.53.0)** (`SpireShape.chest`, 50% by seed, independent of the encounter): a vanilla chest (`StructurePiece.createChest`, facing north) with `dreadpatch:chests/spire_summit` (`SpirePiece.SUMMIT_LOOT`; ours: 4–7 of dread shards, bones, packed ice, Rimefrost scales, iron, gold, diamonds, rare and epic ink; a Gravemaster's Key at 30%, a way home; a Rimefrost dragonforge core at 5% (0.59.0); the music disc at 10% (0.60.0); one of her spells' scrolls at 20%, quality 0.2–0.6, of the lesser ones only (bolt, crown, Barrow-Step, phantoms, Rimefire Blast; Whiteout Dominion, Summon Court and Create Rimefrost Dragon stay in her loot)) at `chestAt()`: the middle, or beside the dais (0, +2) on an encounter spire. A third of them (`lich`) have an Ice and Fire Dread Lich at the chest's south-east, risen with `finalizeSpawn(STRUCTURE)` and kept (`setPersistenceRequired`).
- **Arrival** (`Dreadlands.arrival`, `magic/ArrivalRules`; 0.61.0, the owner: "somewhere random, but never on a tower or within 48 blocks from one. And prefer the surface, but caves or similar are ok"; until then 28–36 blocks from the nearest spire's middle, facing it): up to 24 spots spread evenly within 256 blocks of the gate's x/z (ours), each clear of every spire by 48 past the most any reaches (`SpireShape.MAX_REACH` 21, so 69 from a spire's middle: `findNearestMapStructure(#dreadpatch:spires, the spot, 6 chunks)`, no chunk generated); the first whose surface (`MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES`, then down through the snow layer the biome lays on everything, or a plant) has a dry, sturdy floor and room to stand, its chunk generated; failing every one, the first dry floor lower down under a clear spot (a cave, once there are caves); failing that, the first clear spot's surface. Facing anywhere. `/dreadpatch dreadlands arrival [x z]` says where, and how far from a spire.
- **Home** (`Dreadlands.goHome`): to `DreadlandsData`'s origin for the player (`data/dreadpatch_dreadlands.dat` in the overworld), else the overworld's spawn on its surface. Dying there respawns as anywhere.
- **Its phantoms** (`Dreadlands.onLevelTick`, `LevelTickEvent.Post` in the Dreadlands): every 600 ticks, not on Peaceful, with `doMobSpawning`, each player (not spectating) under open sky has a 1-in-8 chance of 1–2 dread phantoms 20–34 blocks over them, ±10 aside, in valid empty air, never past 4 wild ones within 64. `MobSpawnType.NATURAL`, no summoner, so they hunt players. Not tied to `TIME_SINCE_REST`.

## Flames in the Dreadlands (`world/DreadFlames`, `DreadlandsData`; 0.57.0)

- **The owner** (from a village screenshot): "lanters should be dread variants. Any lanterns placed fade from lanter to soul lantern to dread lanterns. Same with Torches."
- **The dread lantern** (`dreadpatch:dread_lantern`, ours; Ice and Fire has a dread torch but no lantern): a vanilla `LanternBlock`, the soul lantern's properties copied (`ofFullCopy`: light 10, which can't melt snow), its block and item sprites the soul lantern's recoloured by `dreadpatch:dread_recolor` (nothing copied), vanilla's lantern templates; its models `"render_type": "minecraft:cutout"` (0.58.0: vanilla sets its lanterns' cutout in code, so ours drew solid and the chain links' holes came out black); drops itself (pickaxe); Functional Blocks tab; no recipe.
- **Fading:** a lantern, torch or wall torch (vanilla or soul) placed by an entity in the Dreadlands (`BlockEvent.EntityPlaceEvent`) is kept in `DreadlandsData.fading` (packed pos → game time; saved as `Fading`) and steps every `flame_fade_min_seconds`–`flame_fade_max_seconds` (30–45 s since 0.58.0; 600–1,200 ticks before) lantern → soul lantern → dread lantern, torch → soul torch → Ice and Fire's `dread_torch`, wall torch → soul wall torch → `dread_torch_wall`, its shape kept (`shaped`), with smoke and a fizz; checked every 20 ticks, only in loaded chunks. `/setblock` fires no place event, so the smoke test can't check it; the owner saw both kinds fade as they should (0.57.0).
- **Villages:** `DreadVillageStructure.dread` turns lanterns and torches straight to the Dread's (`DreadFlames.dread`); the smoke test finds none of vanilla's left.

## The abandoned villages (`world/DreadVillageStructure`; 0.53.0)

- **The structure** (`dreadpatch:dread_village`, its own `StructureType`): vanilla's `JigsawStructure` is final, so ours holds one (codec field `village`, its own settings repeated) and hands it `findGenerationPoint`. Its start pool `dreadpatch:dread_village/town_centers` is vanilla's three **zombie** snowy town centres (weights 2 : 1 : 3, as in vanilla's pool), whose jigsaws lead only into vanilla's `village/snowy/zombie/*` pools: zombie villagers, mossy and cracked stone, no golems. Biome tag `has_structure/dread_village` (the Dreadlands); `structure_set/dread_villages.json` random spread 40 / 16 chunks, 4 chunks clear of a spire (`exclusion_zone`). Vanilla's overworld villages are untouched.
- **Dread blocks** (`afterPlace`, which runs in each chunk once its pieces are down, over the village's whole box ±4 and 16 over it in that chunk: its trees are feature pieces a block in size and grow out of them, and the first smoke run found their spruce trunks left): planks (`#planks`) → dreadwood planks; logs (`#logs`, stripped too) → dreadwood log, axis kept; cobblestone → dreadstone; stone bricks → dreadstone bricks; mossy cobblestone and mossy bricks → mossy dreadstone bricks; cracked → cracked; cobblestone and stone brick stairs and slabs (and the mossy and plain stone ones) → dreadstone bricks' (every shared property copied: facing, half, shape, type, waterlogged). Leaves (`#leaves`) go, so its trees stand as dead dreadwood trunks. Wood's other shapes (`#minecraft:wooden_stairs`, `wooden_slabs`, `wooden_fences`, `fence_gates`, `wooden_doors`, `wooden_trapdoors`, `wooden_buttons`, `wooden_pressure_plates`; not ours) become dreadwood's (0.65.0; spruce's stayed until there were any), their state copied.
- **Its chests** are vanilla's with vanilla's village tables, so Lootr takes them as it takes any.
- **Its zombie villagers** are vanilla's, and burn by day as anywhere.

## Lootr (0.53.0)

- **Every Dreadlands chest is a plain chest with a loot table** (the summit's, the villages'), and nothing of ours touches Lootr: Lootr (in the owner's pack, 1.11.38) replaces any such chest as the chunk generates, so each player gets their own loot. No dependency, no API.
- **In the dev runs** Lootr 1.11.38.127, the pack's own file, is `localRuntime` (CLAUDE.md, Building and dependencies), and the smoke test sees `lootr:lootr_chest` where a plain chest would be, in a village and on a summit, and no plain chest left.

## What rises in the Dreadlands (`world/DreadlandsSpawns`, `entity/DreadPatrols`, `magic/DreadlandsSpawning`, `world/Dreadlands`; 0.52.0)

- **Ours, the numbers** (each a server config value, read where it's used, so the config screen applies them at once): every 200 ticks each player (not spectating), not on Peaceful, with `doMobSpawning`, has a one-in-N chance, N 12 clear, 6 in snow (`isRaining`), 5 in thunder (`isThundering`: the owner's "heavy snowstorm", "slightly higher"), of 1-3 of her court's kinds (thrall 3 : ghoul 2 : knight 1 : scuttler 1) rising (through `EventHooks.finalizeMobSpawn`, `NATURAL`, so with their own rise and gear) round a spot 24-48 blocks off on open, dry ground in a loaded chunk; never past 8 `DreadMobEntity` within 64. No commander, not persistent: they despawn as monsters do. Biome spawners stay empty: vanilla's would need darkness, and the owner wanted day and night.
- **Stray undead** (0.61.0, the owner's dump: "Add vanilla undead and cold variants (Skeletons, Strays, Zombies, etc.) to the Dreadlands spawn pool at low weights"; ours, our spawner rather than the biome's: a mob type's spawn rule applies in every dimension, and vanilla's monster cap would crowd out the Dread): on the same 200-tick roll, one in `undead_spawns` (16) a player of 1–2 (stray 3 : zombie 2 : skeleton 2 : zombie villager 1, `DreadlandsSpawning.undead`) on open ground 24–48 off, through `finalizeMobSpawn` (`NATURAL`: their own gear), never past `undead_cap` (4) non-Dread `#minecraft:undead` monsters within 64. They despawn as monsters do; the sun there doesn't burn them (below); the Dread fight them, as anything living.
- **Patrols:** every 2,400 ticks, one in 3 a player, unless a patrol's leader is within 128: a dread knight (`finalizeMobSpawn` mounts it on a dread horse, which is discarded: mounted it'd outrun the rest) and 2-4 more of the court's kinds but knights, 32-48 blocks off. Persistent data: `dreadpatch:patrol_leader` (the leader's UUID, on all), `dreadpatch:patrol_waypoint` (int array, on the leader). `PatrolGoal` (goal 4, added on `EntityJoinLevelEvent`, only while it has no live target): the leader walks to its waypoint (its first passing by the player's ground ±16), then picks one 64-128 off; the rest walk to the leader when more than 6 off; a member whose leader is gone leads. Patrols keep Ice and Fire's own targeting: they fight what the Dread fight, then walk on.
- **Phantoms near spires:** the phantom spawner's chance is one in 3 instead of 8 within 96 of a spire's middle (`Dreadlands.nearSpire`: `findNearestMapStructure` within 7 chunks).

## The summit encounter (`world/SpireEncounters`, `magic/EncounterRules`, `DreadPatchConfig`; 0.47.0)

- **Calling it** (`LevelTickEvent.Post` in the Dreadlands, every 20 ticks): for each player (not spectating; creative calls it too, though she can't target them), `StructureManager.getStructureWithPieceAt(pos, #dreadpatch:spires)` gives the spire whose piece box holds them; on an encounter spire's summit (`SpireShape.onSummit`: within its top radius + 2, from a block below its top to 8 over), not on Peaceful:
  - if a queen of that spire is about (persistent data `dreadpatch:spire` = its base, packed; alive within 256 of the summit), they've met it (recorded, unless they had already within the reset);
  - else, if they haven't met it within `encounter_reset_hours` (server config, default 6; real time, `System.currentTimeMillis`), she's called and they've met it.
- **The record:** `DreadlandsData` keeps each spire's (base, packed) last meeting with each player.
- **Her coming:** `EventHooks.finalizeMobSpawn(EVENT)`, so she's the boss with her ancient Syndrigossa, placed 12 past the rim (`summitRadius`, the flared one, since 0.53.0) and 16 over the summit (an ancient Syndrigossa taking wing sinks about 18 before she holds) where a probe ancient Syndrigossa fits (`noCollision`), straight away from the player first, then 45° either way and round; else on the summit on foot (`setWithDragon(false)`). Her target is the player. A visual lightning bolt on the summit, the Wither's spawn sound, an action-bar line to everyone within 48.
- **Taking wing:** `DreadQueen.riseWithDragon` calls the dragon's `takeOff` (Ice and Fire's hovering, then flight) when two blocks under her are air; on the ground (a gate, a spawn egg) it doesn't. Ice and Fire's dragon in the air without it would just fall.
- **Her fall** (`LivingDeathEvent`, not cancelled, in the Dreadlands, a queen with `dreadpatch:spire`): on the ground under where she fell (`moveToRelativeGroundLevel`, 64 down) a frame of dreadstone bricks 5 wide and 6 tall along x, its 3×4 opening and a block either side of it cleared, opened home by `DreadGate.openReturn` for `RETURN_TICKS` (6,000: ours, 5 minutes, for her drops and her dragon's corpse), fading over its last 5 s like any gate. Its frame stays.

## The Dreadlands' storms (`world/DreadlandsStorms`, `client/DreadlandsClient`, `block/RimefireBlock`, `magic/StormRules`; 0.48.0)

- **A storm is any snowfall there** (ours; the owner said "in a storm"): the overworld's rain, read through `DerivedLevelData`, so `level.isRaining()` in the Dreadlands. Lightning only when it's also thundering.
- **Rimefrost** (server, `LevelTickEvent.Post`, every 20 ticks while it snows; since 0.56.0 Rimefrost I, −10%, in plain snow and II, −25%, in thunder, the owner: "normal snow should be a modest 10%. Heavy Should be the current"; its modifier on NeoForge's level curve (`addAttributeModifier(..., Int2DoubleFunction)`; vanilla's is level × amount, and overriding `createModifiers` does nothing, since applying reads the templates): I is `SLOW_MILD`, II and up `SLOW`; phantoms' bites and rimefire give II, `HEAVY`): every living thing in the level that isn't of the Dread or spectating, with the sky over its eyes (`canSeeSky` at eye height), gets `RIMEFROST_TICKS` (60) of Rimefrost, ambient. No damage. Her full regalia eases it a step (`regalia_eases_weather`, 0.58.0: thunder slows as plain snow, plain snow not at all).
- **The fog** (`DreadlandsClient`): `ViewportEvent.RenderFog`, far plane down to `FOG_FAR_SNOW` (56) in plain snow, eased to `FOG_FAR` (24) by the thunder level (0.55.1; 24 in any snow before, and the owner found plain snow "just as thick"), in a sphere, scaled by the snow (`getRainLevel`) and by the camera being under the open sky, eased over 40 ticks; it only ever lowers the distance, so Whiteout's 10 still wins. Fog colour eased to storm grey × sky light. Snowflakes blow past on a wind that turns with the day (three times as many and twice as fast in a whiteout, 0.54.0). No extra wind sound but a whiteout's and the wastes' gale's: the ambience's loop is already the soul sand valley's.
- **By biome** (0.69.0, the owner: "Could the Chasma biome be hazier a little bit. The other biome should have higher winds"; ours: "the other" read as the Sastrugi Wastes, named for wind-cut snow): both under the open sky, eased over 40 ticks by the camera's biome. **The Rime Chasma's haze**, in any weather: the fog's far plane down to `HAZE_FAR` (112) and its near plane to `HAZE_NEAR` (12), so it thickens with distance from close by; the snow's fog closes in over it. **The Sastrugi Wastes' gale:** 4 flakes a tick blow past even in clear weather, and every flake (a storm's too) gusts twice as fast; Whiteout Dominion's wind loop plays at `WhiteoutClient.windVolume`: 0.3 × 0.9 in clear weather, twice that in their snow (`GALE_WIND`). Not a storm, so it doesn't muffle sounds (`windStrength`, which the muffling reads, is only a storm's).
- **Lightning** (`DreadlandsStorms.strike`): vanilla's never strikes there (above, The Dreadlands: time and weather), so in thunder each player has a 1-in-`STRIKE_ONE_IN` (400) chance a tick of a strike within 48 blocks, on `MOTION_BLOCKING` (onto the snow layer). It's a **visual-only bolt** (so no vanilla fire) plus `thunderHit` on everything in vanilla's reach round it but the Dread, then `ignite`: a rimefire patch (`StormRules.patch`: the strike's block and 70% of the rest of a radius-2.5 disc), each on the first spot from 2 above to 3 below that's air or replaceable (snow layers), without fluid, that rimefire survives on.
- **Rimefire since 0.55.0** (the owner: "strictly an environmental and combat hazard for now and cannot be harvested or lit by players", "a maximum light level of 6 so it will not melt surrounding snow or ice", "a dim dread-fire cyan light" (vanilla light has no colour: its cyan is its flames' and embers'), "non-Dread targets take damage"; ours: `RimefireBlock.DAMAGE` 1 a second as `dreadpatch:rimefire`, tagged as `whiteout` is (freezing, past armour, a Rimefrost kind, so her regalia cuts it and its full set cancels it), death message `death.attack.dreadpatch.rimefire`). ## Sound filters (`client/SoundFilters`, `magic/SoundRules`; 0.64.0)

- **The owner:** "Dread weather severity should muffle sounds around the player faster the further the source is away"; of the music, "clip the top end a tiny bit. Do that on all sounds actually".

## The sky and the plains (`client/DreadlandsSky`, `world/DreadTerrain.GravePatch`; 0.67.0)

- **The owner:** "Dim the sunlight to never brighter than 6, and the suns texture/brightness map reflects this"; the dump's "sparse Wither Roses, and sparse gnarled dreadwood trees with rare dread leaf blocks" on the plains.
- **The lightmap:** the dimension type's `effects` is `dreadpatch:dreadlands` (`RegisterDimensionSpecialEffectsEvent`; the overworld's effects otherwise). Its `adjustLightmapColors` (NeoForge's, after vanilla's colour for each block light × sky light entry) takes away the sky's colour over what sky level 6 gives, for every level over 6: vanilla's sky vector (its sky darkening's (d, d, 1) lerped 0.35 to white) times the difference in `LightTexture.getBrightness`. Drawing only: sky light itself (spawning, melting, the undead's sun) is vanilla's.
- **The sun:** while the camera's level is the Dreadlands, vanilla's `textures/environment/sun.png` is registered over with a `DynamicTexture` of itself, each pixel's brightest channel × 0.42 in pale cyan (0.62, 0.93, 1); the sun's drawn additively, so darker is dimmer. Released (vanilla's reloads) on leaving, and re-made after a resource reload. Shader packs draw their own sky and light.
- **Grave patches** (`dreadpatch:grave_patch`, the plains only, vegetal decoration, one chunk in 6, on `WORLD_SURFACE_WG`): the top blocks within 1-2 of its foot (solid, open above, 85% each) to graveyard soil, 1-3 wither roses on it (graveyard soil is in `#minecraft:dirt`, so a rose takes it), and at 40% a dead dreadwood tree: 3-6 logs, a sideways stub of a branch, 0-2 leaves by its top.
