# Gear: dragonscale, dread dragonsteel, her regalia, trims

Part of the notes for agents working here: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) has the rules and the index.

## Rimefrost dragonscale and dread dragonsteel (`registry/DreadGear`, `item/DreadArmorItem`, `item/DragonArmor`, `item/DreadDragonsteel`, `loot/RimefrostScalesFunction`, `client/DreadRecolors`, `magic/DreadRecolor`; 0.42.0)

- **Items:** `rimefrost_dragon_scale`, `rimefrost_dragon_blood`, `dread_dragonsteel_ingot`; `rimefrost_dragonscale_*` and `dread_dragonsteel_*` armour; dread dragonsteel sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel and hoe.
- **Getting them:**
  - Rimefrost corpses (Syndrigossa's too) drop the scale in place of Ice and Fire's coloured scales. Their count is sized by `dreadpatch:rimefrost_scales` as Ice and Fire's `dragon_loot` sizes its own: age / 25 + a roll up to age / 5. `dragon_loot` itself only counts `DragonScalesItem`s and swaps them to the dragon's colour.
  - Ice and Fire's dragonforge takes recipes from data (`iceandfire:dragonforge`: `dragonType`, `cookTime`, `input`, `blood`, `result`). Ours: scale + bottle → blood (750), as Ice and Fire's `ice_dragon_blood`; iron + blood → ingot (1,000): `ice` until 0.58.0, made in Ice and Fire's ice forge; `rimefrost` since 0.59.0, made only in ours ([dragons.md](dragons.md), The Rimefrost dragonforge).
  - Crafting copies Ice and Fire's patterns, with tools on `#c:bones/wither`. Recipe-book unlocks on holding the scale or ingot.
- **Numbers:**
  - Scale armour is Ice and Fire's dragonscale material: 5/7/9/5, enchantability 15, toughness 2, durability 397/577/541/469.
  - Dread dragonsteel armour reads Ice and Fire's config (`armors.dragonsteel*`) as its own does.
  - Tools: `DreadDragonsteel.TIER` delegates to `IafTiers.DRAGONSTEEL_ICE`, repaired with our ingot, on Ice and Fire's own `ActivePostHit*Item` classes with its ice tools' attack numbers (sword +3/−2.4, pickaxe +1/−2.8, axe +5/−3, shovel +1.5/−3, hoe −4/0).
- **Abilities:**
  - `DreadDragonsteel.RIME` (a `PostHitAbility`) leaves 60 ticks of Withering Rime. **Ice and Fire runs these from the item's `hurtEnemy`, which only a player's attack calls** (vanilla `Mob.doHurtTarget` never does), so its own abilities are players' only. Ours also rimes on a mob's `mob_attack` with one (`LivingDamageEvent.Post`).
  - **Against breath** (`DragonArmor`), Ice and Fire's rule (its `ServerEvents.onEntityDamage`): per piece 0.1/0.3/0.2/0.1 off dragon breath (`IafDamageTypes.DRAGON_*_TYPE`), in `LivingDamageEvent.Pre`, after armour. Ice and Fire applies it for its armour against its breath; we apply it for ours against its breath, and for its and ours against `withering_breath` and `shatter_spine`.
- **Looks** (nothing of Ice and Fire's ships):
  - item sprites come from a sprite source of ours (`dreadpatch:dread_recolor`, registered on `RegisterSpriteSourceTypesEvent`, listed in `assets/minecraft/atlases/blocks.json`). Each entry loads Ice and Fire's sprite through NeoForge's `SpriteResourceLoader.loadSprite(..., SpriteContentsConstructor)` and recolours it (`DreadRecolor`, game-free: soot, slate, ash, bone by brightness, cold bright blue to her cyan; `NativeImage` keeps pixels ABGR);
  - item models copy Ice and Fire's (the sword and axe have its large-texture display transforms);
  - armour is drawn with Ice and Fire's `IceDragonScaleArmorModel` / `DragonSteelIceArmorModel` (public, standalone; Ice and Fire hands its own to Uranus' `IArmorRendererBase.RENDERERS`, ours go through NeoForge's `IClientItemExtensions.getHumanoidArmorModel`), in layer textures recoloured from Ice and Fire's (`dragon_scales_white_layer_*`, `dragonsteel_ice_layer_*`) as `DynamicTexture`s the first time they're drawn (`DreadArmorItem.getArmorTexture` → `DreadArmorItem.textures`, set by the client), remade on a resource reload.

**Against webs** (`effect/WebEase`, `magic/WebRules`; 0.75.0, the owner's Feature_Dump_11-24: "Dread armor sets partially reduce web effects rather than granting full immunity so webs preserve tactical counterplay"): each `DreadArmorItem` worn (Rimefrost dragonscale, dread dragonsteel, her regalia) eases a web's hold by 12.5%, 50% for four: before each tick (`EntityTickEvent.Pre`, both sides, since a player moves on its client) an entity standing in a `WebBlock` or `DreadWebBlock` has its `stuckSpeedMultiplier` (by reflection) moved that share toward 1 (a cobweb's 0.25 to 0.625 across, 0.05 to 0.525 up and down). Only webs: powder snow and berry bushes hold as ever.

## Her regalia (`registry/DreadGear`, `client/QueenArmorModels`, `entity/QueenRegalia`, `magic/RegaliaDrops`; 0.43.0)

- **Items:** `dread_queen_crown`, `_bodice`, `_robe`, `_greaves` ("... of the Dreaded Queen", after Ice and Fire's "Blade/Staff of the Dreaded Queen"), `DreadArmorItem`s of `Kind.QUEEN`, epic, fire-resistant.
- **Numbers:** material `dread_queen`: dread dragonsteel's (Ice and Fire's `armors.dragonsteel*` config, durability too), netherite's equip sound, repaired with our ingot. A `DreadArmorItem`, so `DragonArmor` gives it the per-piece breath cut and it carries the dragon-armour tooltip.
- **Model** (0.81.0, `QueenArmorModels`, `RegaliaGeometry`; the owner's three pictures, asked: "Redraw armour and queen"): `tools/models/make_regalia.py` (with `regalia_armour.py`) holds it once and writes `client/RegaliaGeometry` (a humanoid a piece, every bare part empty, `<part>_regalia` children carrying its boxes, vanilla's `CubeDeformation` growth: 1 outer, 0.5 the leggings) and `textures/entity/regalia.png` (256 × 128, armour and wings, cut out), and with `--preview DIR` renders a figure in it all, front, back, sides, top and under, to set against the pictures. `DreadRecolors.armorTexture` gives that sheet for `Kind.QUEEN`.
  - crown: a dark helm (grown 1) open at the face under a cyan brow, a nasal bar; a crown band and ice spikes round its top, tallest at the front's middle;
  - bodice: the breastplate (cyan V at the collar, a line down its middle), belt and cyan buckle; vambraces banded cyan; pauldrons crusted with frost, three frost spikes each;
  - robe: navy leggings (0.5, a cyan stripe), and on each leg (so it swings with it) a tabard half before and behind and a side flap, dark blue, frost tongues up its edges, a ragged frosted hem;
  - greaves: dark (grown 1), a cyan band, steel toe caps;
  - the winged bodice's wings (`chest_winged`): on the body, at (±2, 2.5, 3.2), each a sheet 30 × 28 (cut out: three finger bones from a wrist, the membrane scalloped between them, frosted at its edge, icicles off it) and a leading bone. `QueenArmorModels.poseWings` folds them back and down at rest and spreads them while the wearer `isFallFlying`, each time the armour layer asks for the model. **0.81.2** (the owner, in the client: "stuck going forward until gliding. They go out for gliding but do nothing else animated. Look at how the winged cursium set handles this"; then "if azurelib makes a better visual include it as a dependency": it wouldn't, so not): each wing three segments of 10 (`right_wing`, `_mid`, `_tip`, each hinged on the last; the membrane painted across all three), after Cataclysm: Spellbooks' Cursium mage elytra (AzureLib keyframes: idle, fall_fly, flying), done in code: at rest folded like an accordion behind the back (root 1.1 back, middle −2.1, tip 1.9; the sign that pointed them forward fixed), breathing (the root ±10° at 0.218 rad a tick, Cursium's 250°/s); gliding spread, tilted back 0.52, bobbing ±5° and each segment flexing ±5° at Cursium's three beats; a creative player's flight beating once every 22 ticks (Cursium's 1.08 s), down and up, each segment lagging; eased between them (`WING_EASE` 0.18 a tick, each wearer's own, a weak map). `make_regalia.py --preview` renders it folded (`figure_folded_*`) and spread.
  - Trims go on vanilla's shape (`TrimmedArmorModel`), as before. Until 0.81.0 it was her own outfit's boxes in Ice and Fire's texture of her.
- **Her winged bodice** (0.81.0, the owner's pictures: "the optional elytra version crafted with the elytra, armor and rimefrost or ice dragon scale"; asked: "Bodice + elytra + scale"): `dread_queen_winged_bodice`, a `WingedBodiceItem` (a `DreadArmorItem` of `Kind.QUEEN`, so a piece of the set everywhere it's counted), gliding by NeoForge's `canElytraFly`/`elytraFlightTick` (unbreakable, so flight never wears it). A smithing transform (`recipe/dread_queen_winged_bodice.json`): template `#dreadpatch:winged_bodice_scales` (the Rimefrost scale and Ice and Fire's blue, white, sapphire and silver ones), base the bodice, addition an elytra; components carry over. Unlocked by holding the bodice. `DreadGear.regalia()` (the four and it) for unbreakability, the creative tab and the armour model; `DreadGear.isBodice` for There Must Always Be a Dread Queen; the advancements' bodice criteria take `#dreadpatch:regalia_bodices`. Not in `#minecraft:trimmable_armor`, and never dropped (`queenArmor()`, the four, still decides drops).
- **Her sword and staff redrawn** (0.81.0, `client/RegaliaWeapons`; asked: "Yes, 3D models"): voxel item models from `make_regalia.py` (`models/item/dread_queen_sword_regalia.json`, `_staff_regalia`; one palette texture, `item/regalia_palette`, each box one colour, the cyan ones lit full by NeoForge's per-element `block_light`/`sky_light`), registered as standalone models and put in place of `iceandfire:dread_queen_sword`/`_staff`'s inventory models on `ModelEvent.ModifyBakingResult`, so nothing of Ice and Fire's is overwritten. Their held transforms are a first guess, for a client to settle. 0.81.2: a model that fails to load (it bakes as the missing model) is no longer swapped in, so Ice and Fire's stays. 0.81.1: the staff's shaft two pixels shorter (it ran to −18, past the −16 an item model may reach, so the game refused it and drew Ice and Fire's), and the script checks the bounds.
- **Drops** (`QueenRegalia`, `LivingDropsEvent`): only a queen a `ServerPlayer` killed (the source's entity, so their arrows too). Which piece is `RegaliaDrops.pick` (game-free, tested): a missing piece, plus a missing second at 10% a Looting level; with the set, one at random at 25% + 5% a Looting level. Not in her loot table, which can't see advancements.
- **Advancements** (`data/dreadpatch/advancement/regalia/`): `root` (a player killed a queen), `crown`/`bodice`/`robe`/`greaves` (`inventory_changed`, so held once), `complete` (all four, held at any time; a challenge). `QueenRegalia` reads the four pieces' as what a player has.
- **Icons:** `make_icons.py` (`queen_crown` …), symmetric pixel art in her texture's colours.
- **"There Must Always Be a Dread Queen"** (`regalia/enthroned`; the owner, 0.44.0: "equipping all dread queen items at once"; ours: the four pieces and her sword and staff, one in each hand, either way round). The owner: "upon equip the check is performed on all slots then and only then". So it's not a `tick` criterion: `QueenRegalia.onEquip`, on `LivingEquipmentChangeEvent` (raised by vanilla's own equipment comparison when a slot changes, the held hotbar slot included), checks every slot only when the item put in is one of the six (0.44.1; the owner found checking on any change "way too aggressive"), never once it's earned, and awards its `impossible` criterion.

**The regalia's powers** (`item/DreadRegalia`, `magic/RegaliaBonuses`; 0.51.0):
- **Indestructible:** `unbreakable` set as a default component (`ModifyDefaultComponentsEvent`), so stacks already made have it.
- **Each piece:** +150 Iron's max mana (`ItemAttributeModifierEvent`, in its own slot); 5% off an Ice or Rimefrost spell's mana (`SpellOnCastEvent`, players); 5% more Rimefrost damage dealt by the wearer (its causing entity, or for Withering Rime's withering, which has none, whoever laid the rime: `RimeSiphon.layer`; before armour: `LivingIncomingDamageEvent`; the owner: "both ways on what counts as rimefrost"); 5% off Rimefrost damage taken, after armour (`LivingDamageEvent.Pre`). Rimefrost damage, both ways: `#dreadpatch:rimefrost` (`withering_breath`, `shatter_spine`, `whiteout`, `rimefrost_magic`) and vanilla `wither` damage on someone under Withering Rime and not vanilla Wither (Withering Rime's withering is vanilla's wither damage). It stacks with the dragon-armour breath cut (her breath: 70% off, then 20%).
- **Her crown's sight** (`client/CrownSight`; 0.58.0; 0.64.0, the owner: it didn't seem to touch her storm's fog: a Whiteout Dominion's drawn snow, wall and haze are thinned by the same factor, `WhiteoutRenderer` dividing its strength by `whiteoutReach`): worn, it keeps the Dreadlands' storm fog off its wearer (`crown_storm_sight`; `DreadlandsClient`'s fog skips it) and lets them see `crown_whiteout_sight` further in any whiteout (`WhiteoutClient`'s fog, times 1 + it). A value set by command reaches a multiplayer client only when it next joins (server configs sync on login).
- **The full set:** `setTicksFrozen(0)` each tick and freezing damage (`#minecraft:is_freezing`) cancelled; the Dread (`IDreadMob`) can't take the wearer as a target (`LivingChangeTargetEvent`, and each second a held one is dropped), except the queen, Syndrigossa, anything whose commander (a dread mob's `CommanderUUID`, a phantom's summoner, otherwise `getCommander`) is one of them, and one the wearer hurt in the last 600 ticks (persistent data `dreadpatch:provoked_by` / `_until`). Any living wearer, not only players. It also eases the Dreadlands' weather slow a step (`regalia_eases_weather`; [dreadlands.md](dreadlands.md), storms).

## Armour trims (`client/TrimmedArmorModel`; 0.44.0)

- **The owner:** "Enable trim support for the dread queens armor, dread scale and dread dragon steel armors." Ice and Fire's own armour isn't trimmable (no tag), so this is ours only.
- **The tag:** all twelve pieces in `#minecraft:trimmable_armor` (`data/minecraft/tags/item/`), so the smithing table takes them.
- **Icons:** each armour item model has vanilla's ten `trim_type` overrides (quartz 0.1 … amethyst 1.0), each a `<item>_<material>_trim` model with vanilla's paletted `minecraft:trims/items/<type>_trim_<material>` as `layer1` (in the block atlas already). Trim materials other mods add show no overlay unless they add their own overrides.
- **Worn:** the armour layer draws a trim through whatever model the item hands it (NeoForge's `renderTrim(..., Model, ...)`), reading the trim's 64×32 vanilla-layout sprite through that model's texture coordinates; ours are laid out for her 128×64 sheet and Ice and Fire's 64×64 armour, so a trim came out scrambled. Every one of our armour models is wrapped in a `TrimmedArmorModel`: vanilla's armour shape (`PLAYER_OUTER_ARMOR`, or `_INNER_` for leggings) carrying the real model. Its `renderToBuffer` draws on the vanilla shape when the buffer is a `SpriteCoordinateExpander` (vanilla's trim pass always goes through `TextureAtlasSprite.wrap`), and otherwise (the armour, its glint) copies its pose and part visibility to the real model and draws that. No mixin.
