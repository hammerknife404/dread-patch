# Testing

Part of the notes for agents working here: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) has the rules and the index.

## Unit tests

`./gradlew test`, part of `build`:
- **Rules:** one test per class in `magic/`.
- **Client and data:**
  - `UndeadSkinTest`;
  - `IceSpikeTest` (spike geometry, through a `VertexConsumer`);
  - `RisePoseTest`;
  - `DreadPhantomSkinTest` and `QueenArmorModelsTest` (bakes each regalia slot's model);
  - `SaddleSeatTest` and `SyndrigossaAnimationsTest` (against Ice and Fire's files on the test classpath).
- **`DreadQueenModelTest`:**
  - bakes her layer and builds the `HumanoidModel` from it, so a missing part fails here, not in a client;
  - checks that her bust's texels are opaque.
- **Limits:** game classes are on the test classpath (`addModdingDependenciesTo(sourceSets.test)`), but there's no running game.

## Dev server smoke test

**Running it**
- `tools/devserver/queen_smoke.py` (Python 3, stdlib) drives `./gradlew runServer` over RCON, and stops the server when done (`--keep` leaves it up).
- It needs `enable-rcon=true`, `rcon.password=<anything>` and `server-ip=127.0.0.1` in `run/server.properties` (gitignored; already set on the owner's machine).

**What it learned**
- **NoAI mobs still pick up loot** (2026-11-27): `canPickUpLoot` runs in `aiStep`, which NoAI doesn't stop, so thralls summoned to read their hands took a dread beast's bones dropped earlier; summon such mobs with `CanPickUpLoot:0b`. And a risen Dread Fish takes her target and may leap 7 out of its hole (0.86.0) before it's looked for: look within 10.
- **Measure a column once its chunk is done** (0.84.1): a lone chunk forceloaded and measured at once (`positioned over world_surface`) gave a top 1-6 blocks over its finished one, so the check found air there; the glacier check now loads every point, waits 8 s, then measures, as the chasma's does.
- **The Glacial Plains cut the others up** (0.84.0): 40% of the land under the glacier leaves fewer chasma points on a coarse grid round where `locate` finds one (its edge), so searches that hunt a biome's middle need more tries. Probing tops on a grid (11 × 11, 48 apart, the biome at each) showed the chasma's floor still at 30-31 inside it, the glacier's top at 194-198, its cliff standing about 145 blocks over the wastes beside it, one probe apart.
- **It catches crashes** (0.83.0): a Dread Beast eating an item another had just emptied drew crumbs from an empty stack, which throws and crashed the server's tick; the run stopped with "RCON connection closed". Read `run/crash-reports/` when a run stops early.
- **A raised dragon can block the next raising's ray** (0.83.0): full grown, the first Rimefrost dragon stood over where the second corpse is summoned, shoving it or standing across the spell's ray (which stops at the first thing it meets) now and then; the test moves it away before the second corpse lies down. And (0.83.0) her storm's risen beasts wander off to eat what the dead dropped, so a check that nothing stands somewhere counts before and after.
- **Item model bounds** (0.81.1): vanilla refuses a whole item model with any element past −16..32 on an axis (`BlockElement` "'from' specifier exceeds the allowed boundaries"), and the item keeps its old model; only a client's log shows it (grep `Unable to load model: 'dreadpatch:`). 0.81.0's staff reached −18. `make_regalia.py` now refuses to write one.
- **The watchdog** (0.77.0): forceloading many lone chunks far apart, back to back, can hold one tick past 60 s (each waits for its neighbours) and the server shuts itself down; pause between them.
- **Never count water by swapping it to sponge** (0.77.0): a placed sponge soaks up the water round it, so the count destroys what it counts. The deeps' check swaps to glass.
- **The server stalls generating** (0.77.0): forceloading 256 new chunks can hold the server thread 10 s or more, past `Rcon`'s 10 s timeout; a survey script raised its socket's timeout.
- **No random ticks without a player** (0.76.0): `ServerChunkCache.tickChunks` random-ticks a chunk only when a player's close enough for spawning, forceloaded or not, so a gate's Dread (its panes' random ticks) can't be seen there; `randomTickSpeed` doesn't help.
- **`/reload` first:** Iron's builds its spell config on datapack sync (a player joining, or `/reload`). Before that, every spell reads max level 1.
- **World setup:** it sets noon, because dragons sleep at night and monsters spawn on dark platforms. It sets Easy, and puts the difficulty back at the end.
- **Command pitfalls:**
  - Commands under `execute` need `run`.
  - A check on a failed summon passes vacuously, so summons are checks too.
  - NBT tests go inside a selector's brackets.
  - `has_effect` stops the run on a malformed command, rather than reading it as a fail.
  - With no vehicle, `execute ... on vehicle` prints nothing at all.
- **Timing:**
  - An instant effect from `/effect` lands on the next tick, and so do held items' attributes.
  - NoAI mobs keep the `OnGround` they're summoned with.
  - A wild dragon ages a tick each tick.
