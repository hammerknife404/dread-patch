# Toward 1.0

A review pass (2026-11-28, the owner: "Do a review pass bug review and v1.0 approaches! ... auto fix issues using your best
judgement"): four reviews of the beta's work since 0.97.5 (spells and client; worldgen and blocks; entities and AI) and of the
whole mod for a 1.0 release. What they found and what was done with it.

## Fixed in 0.98.13

- **Fading campfires cooked their food at once:** carried as bare items onto a soul campfire, its cooking times were 0. Now the
  campfire's whole state carries over (`DreadFlames.turn`).
- **The wastes' ponds read two chunks off** (walk 8 + rim 12), where the ground may not be made yet: they hung on the order
  chunks were made. The walk is 4 now, so every read is within 16 of its chunk. Their cut banks left snow floating: cleared.
- **Graveyards landed inside their village** (2-5 chunks; a village reaches 5): now 6-8. Every region in range is sampled (it
  sampled three points, right only while the village spacing was over twice its reach); its chunk counts can't be 0. Ice and
  Fire's graveyard rolls its own `generateGraveYardChance` (0.5 by default), so ours is 1, not a second roll halving it.
- **Leaps into lava or fire:** lava has no collision, so a lava pool read as landing. Now refused.
- **Doors with mob griefing off:** the Dread planned paths through doors they'd never break, and pushed at them. Their door
  pathing now asks griefing too; and only the Dread's own (a risen villager or guard keeps opening doors).
- **Liches and bowmen leapt at the target they stood off from:** they keep away now, as the queen does.
- **The queen's hops** checked no room for their rise and no ground on the way: both now.
- **A Dread Fish making for water slid sideways** while it turned: pushed the way it faces, by how near it faces the water.
  Its head is kept within 75 of its body again (the new body control dropped vanilla's clamp).
- **Lich Skull struck its caster's own mount or pets** (no owner, so vanilla's checks were gone; a dragon's parts too), and
  **skulls steered nearly straight up or down vanished** (Ice and Fire's skull discards itself under 0.1 a tick across): its
  heading's kept at least 0.15 across.
- **The regalia models' missing-model fallback never fired:** a failed model is its own copy of the missing model; now told
  by its sprite.
- **A Dread Campfire saved its chunk every tick**, empty or not: only with food on it.
- **Ice spiders near villages** could be raised inside the ground.
- **A duplicated lang key** (`dreadpatch.configuration.regalia`, the config screen's section).
- **Iron's Spells' floor** raised to 3.16.3, which the code is built against and uses internals of (`SummonManager`,
  `RecastInstance`, its packets): older 3.x would fail at load.
- **A guard:** `ServerSafetyTest` fails the build if any class a dedicated server loads names a client-only class.

- **(0.98.14) Pools refused too often:** a 4-deep column needed solid ground 4 down, and one without refused the whole
  pool; the chasma's floor is full of water pockets, so a smoke run found none in 25 plates. Each column now goes as deep as
  its ground allows, never more than one deeper than a neighbour.
- **(0.98.14) The smoke test's own faults:** the queen's hop check cleared the shared pad (two dozen failures after it), two
  checks built outside the loaded chunks, the tome check misread its data. Three full runs: 545, 564, 567 of 569, the rest
  chance.

## Left for the owner (P0-P1)

- **A real dedicated server.** The dev server runs with the merged jar, so client classes are there and a mistake wouldn't
  show; the new test and a bytecode scan found none. Still: boot the `RELEASE=true` jar once on a NeoForge 21.1.265 server
  with the pack's server mods, into the Dreadlands.
- **The client checks owed** (`testing.md`, Client), Iris/Complementary among them (never checked: the storm fog under shaders
  is the likeliest complaint in the pack).
- **Release text:** the README still says "Patching in the Dread Queen for my own world" and "in development"; a curated
  `.github/release-notes.md` (what 1.0 is, requirements, known issues) instead of dozens of PR lines.
- **Ice and Fire's range** is `[2.1-beta.1,)`, open above, though the mod subclasses its queen and ice dragon and leans on
  internals; its 1.21.1 branch is already 2.2. Cap it, or re-test when 2.2 ships (a cap holds back pack updates: your call).
- **`neoforge.mods.toml`**: no `displayURL`, `logoFile` or `credits` (the queen's model geometry is Ice and Fire's, LGPL).
- **Iron doors in the open:** a knight's path may end short of an iron door that isn't in a corridor (a door's cell is a node,
  but the pathfinder can't sweep past it). The smoke check's corridor passes; worth one test at a house in a client.

## Worth doing (P2)

- `DreadSwarm` walks every entity in every dimension each second; `DreadlandsStorms`/`DreadlandsWhiteout` refresh effects on
  everything under the sky each second (a packet each); `WebEase` reflects on every living entity every tick. All cheap enough
  now; worth narrowing.
- Our `dreadwood_leaves` loot table replaces Ice and Fire's outright; a loot modifier would survive its adding one.
- A player has no in-game trail to her (key, gate, Dreadlands, spire): an advancement trail or a JEI info page.
- The smoke test could fail on any `dreadpatch` error or warning in the server log.
- Trim `CLAUDE.md`'s "Where things stand" at 1.0.
