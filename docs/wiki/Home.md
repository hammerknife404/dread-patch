# Dread Patch

Dread Patch is a NeoForge 1.21.1 mod that puts Ice and Fire CE's unfinished Dread Queen into the game: a necromancer boss with her own spells, court, storm and undead dragon, a gate to summon her, and loot worth the fight.

**Status:** in development. She doesn't spawn naturally yet.

## Pages

- [Installation](Installation): what to install and where.
- [Requirements and Compatibility](Requirements-and-Compatibility): versions, dependencies, what's tested and what isn't.
- [Features](Features): the Dread Queen, Syndrigossa, the Dread Gate, her spells, items and loot.

## Quick start

1. Install the mod and its requirements ([Installation](Installation)).
2. Craft a **Gravemaster's Key**: three Dread Shards and a Bone across the top, a Dread Shard below, two Gold Ingots at the bottom right.
3. Build a frame of dreadstone round an opening 2 to 5 wide and 3 to 7 tall, like a Nether portal, and right-click it with the key. Five seconds later the Dread Queen comes through.

Or use her spawn egg (Spawn Eggs tab) or `/summon dreadpatch:dread_queen`.

## The Dread Lands

Not in the mod yet. Ice and Fire CE has no Dreadlands dimension (old Ice and Fire's portal is gone), and the Dread Gate summons the queen rather than leading anywhere.
