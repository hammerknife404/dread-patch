# Dread Fish

Serpentine dread anglerfish of the Dreadlands' dark water: a big, bone-pale anglerfish head with a cold cyan cast, its jaw hanging open on long fangs, black eyes burning cyan, fanned bony fins and a jagged dorsal fin, and a thick, ribbed serpent's body curling round behind it into a ring. About one in six has a lure: a tall stalk reaching over its snout with a lantern hanging from it, which sparkles while lit, hangs straight down whichever way the fish turns, reaches out while the fish hunts or swims about, and draws in over its head while it's still. The rest have no lure at all. Each comes in its own size, 0.6 to 2.5 blocks across, with 10 to 18 health and a bite of 2 to 4 to match, and 4 armour; one in twenty is an alpha or matron, a **Rime Wyrm**, 2.5 to 4 blocks across, with up to 24.5 health and a bite of 5.5. Spawn one with the **Dread Fish Spawn Egg** (Spawn Eggs tab) or `/summon dreadpatch:dread_fish`.

- **Where:** any dark water in the Dreadlands (two blocks deep at least, light 6 or less), even the scattered pockets sealed in the Rime Chasma's ice ([The Dreadlands](The-Dreadlands)), rarely ([Settings](Settings): `dreadlands_dread_fish`, `cave_dread_water`, `chasma_dread_water`), never more than four near you. They also come up out on the Rime Chasma's open floor now and then (`chasma_surface_fish`), though they'd rather be in water: where there's dark water near, they rise there instead, and out of it they make for the nearest. And anything that lives in water and dies in the queen's storm ([The Dread Queen](The-Dread-Queen)) rises as one of hers.
- **In water and out:** it swims fast and climbs single blocks; out of water it slithers like a snake, never drowns or dries out, and is as keen to get back to water as to chase its prey, whichever it sets out on first.
- **What it hunts:** players and any mob not of the Dread (not dragons or bosses). It's of the Dread: allied with the queen and her court, untouched by Withering Rime, Whiteout and Rimefrost.
- **Its lure:** flickers erratically, off far more often than on. In a fight it pulses every 3 to 5 seconds, laying 4 seconds of Rimefrost (25% slower) or Whiteout on everything within 5 blocks that isn't of the Dread, and lighting up (Glowing, 4 seconds) everything within it but Dread Fish, the Dread included. If the pulse catches anything, the fish goes into a **Frenzy** for 4 seconds and bites twice as fast. Fish without a lure never pulse.
- **Glowing prey:** any fish whose prey is Glowing (from a lure's pulse, a spectral arrow, anything) goes into a Frenzy while it glows, so one lantern fish sets the fish round it on whatever it lights. A fish notices Glowing prey from twice as far off, and goes for it before anything else.
- **It swims like a dolphin:** quick and restless, and near the surface it breaches now and then, leaping out along its way and diving back in.
- **The leap:** from just under the surface it leaps out at prey on the bank or above the water, up to 4 blocks high and 7 across, biting what it touches, or snatching food lying by the water, then waits 3 to 8 seconds before it can leap again. On land it springs at its prey the same way, every 2 seconds.
- **Its bite:** by its size, once a second (twice in a Frenzy), with a 10% chance of 3 seconds of Withering Rime. On land it lunges at its target from up to 5 blocks, then waits 2 seconds before the next.
- **Rime Wyrms fight differently:** heavy natural armour (14, with toughness, and hard to knock back), and a slow bite you can see coming: every 2.5 seconds (1.5 in a Frenzy), it stops, rears back with its jaw gaping and roars, and snaps 0.8 seconds later, missing if you've stepped out of reach. It hits 1.75 times as hard as its size would, with heavy knockback, and lunges half as often.
- **It eats:** most organic things dropped (food, seeds and crops, meat and fish, bones, leather, feathers, string, wool, ink sacs, spider eyes, saplings, leaves, flowers; not golden apples or golden carrots), one item at a time, as Dread Beasts and scuttlers do; only food heals it, 0.25 to 1 health each (rotten flesh 0.5, a steak 1), so it clears out a mob farm's leftovers.
- **Summoned:** the Summon Dread Fish spell calls one a level, and a wild Syndrigossa calls them in her fights ([Spells](Spells), [Rimefrost Dragons](Rimefrost-Dragons)).
- **Its drops:** 1 to 2 bones and 1 to 2 dread shards (up to one more of each a Looting level), now and then a rotten flesh (10%), and rarely (5%) 1 to 2 Rimefrost Dragon Scales.
