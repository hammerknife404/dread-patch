# Other mods

- **Infection mods:** the Dread, the Rimefrost dragons, the queen and the risen never take an infection or body-conversion mod's effects, so they're never infected or converted: Fungal Infection: Spore and The Flesh That Hates by default. The list is the `infection_mods` setting ([Settings](Settings)).
- **Better Mob Combat Reimagined:** its swings reach 2.5 blocks however large the mob, so a dread knight's weapon reaches half again as far, as the knight is; the extra reach comes off it when it drops.
