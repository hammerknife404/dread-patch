# Dread Patch

Patching in the Dread Queen for my own world.

[Ice and Fire: Community Edition](https://modrinth.com/mod/iceandfire-ce) ships a Dread Queen (her class, textures, name, sword and staff) but never puts her in the game. This NeoForge mod finishes her.

**Status:** in development. The Dread Queen exists as `dreadpatch:dread_queen`: spawn her with her spawn egg (Spawn Eggs tab) or `/summon dreadpatch:dread_queen`. She's a lean sovereign: 300 health behind 20 armor and 12 toughness (a full netherite set's), with a boss bar, her sword and staff, and her original model, and she rises out of the ground when spawned. She fights like a necromancer: she keeps her distance, circling at casting range, giving ground when pressed and, when cornered, breaking away with **Barrow-Step**: she steps 10 to 15 blocks away through the cold, leaving a husk of rime-glass in her shape where she stood. Whatever was hunting her turns on the husk, and when it's struck, or after 5 seconds, it shatters, rooting everyone near it for 2 seconds and touching them with Withering Rime. She saves her sword for whoever still reaches her. From range she shoots her **Withering Rime-Bolt**: soot-black, bone-white frost that takes a share of the target's own life on top of its damage and leaves **Withering Rime**, which withers through armor, freezes and stops all healing. Her own Withering Rime feeds her: each second it withers anyone she afflicted (by her bolt, her crown's shrapnel, her sword, her Decree or her husk), she heals 1 on Easy, 2 on Normal and 3 on Hard for each of them, while she's within 32 blocks; they lose 1 a second, as under anyone's Withering Rime, and players' Withering Rime feeds no one. No share of anything's max health she takes, from any spell or her sword, is ever more than 20, so a warden or a dragon is a long fight for her too. She raises a **Rimefire Crown** round her target, a ring of ice spikes wreathed in cold cyan dread fire over cyan and black smoke, that roots everyone inside and drinks every other fire within it, then shatters it with her bolt for a burst of shrapnel. She raises her **Court of the Dread**, Ice and Fire's own thralls, ghouls, knights and scuttlers rising from the ground around her, and with **Beater's Decree** burns one of them from behind as it presses an enemy: a burst of rime that sends the rest of her court into a frenzy. She drops her sword and staff, two epic or legendary inks, and one or two scrolls of her spells (Withering Rime-Bolt, Rimefire Crown, Barrow-Step), Ice spells (they count Ice spell power) found nowhere else. A player's Barrow-Step takes them to where they look (11 to 15 blocks, by level) and leaves their husk behind. Her staff is an Iron's staff now: it carries both her spells at level 3 (on your spell wheel while you hold it, cast with right-click), gives +15% Ice Spell Power and +10% Cooldown Reduction in the main hand, and through its **Permafrost Conduit** makes Ice spells cost 20% less mana while you stand on snow or ice. Her sword is unbreakable, with 11.5 attack damage at 1.6 speed and half a block more reach. Through **Thermodynamic Theft** every strike leaves 4.5 seconds of Withering Rime. Through **Glass-Rime Shatter** every third full strike on the same chilled or frozen foe (each within 5 seconds of the last; hitting others in between doesn't reset it, so it works with Better Combat's sweeping swings) shatters its frost for an extra 10% of its max health past its armor (at most 20) and leaves it **Brittle** (10% less armor) for 4 seconds. Its **Fire-Drinking Guard** rises while you hold right-click, slowing you like a shield: it stops fire and magic from the front (Iron's spells and Ice and Fire's dragon fire included; arrows and blades still get through) and puts out fire on you and around you, and a hit it stops charges the blade, which glints until its next full strike shatters at once. With the sword in your main hand, an off hand holding something with its own right-click (a shield, food, a bow, blocks, throwables, a staff) keeps it, so raise the guard with that hand empty or holding something like a totem. She swings the same sword, so her own strikes leave Withering Rime and shatter too. She doesn't spawn naturally yet. **Syndrigossa**, her undead frost wyrm, is in progress: so far an Ice and Fire ice dragon rotted to the bone (soot-black scales, bone showing through, torn wings), allied with the queen and her court, with a spawn egg in the Spawn Eggs tab. Her breath, drops and stats are still an ice dragon's.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265+, Ice and Fire CE 2.1-beta.1+ (with Uranus, Jupiter and GeckoLib, which it requires anyway), and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 3.x for 1.21.1.

## Building

`./gradlew build` puts the jar in `build/libs/`. `./gradlew runClient` starts a dev client with Ice and Fire loaded.
