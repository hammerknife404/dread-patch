# Dread Patch

Patching in the Dread Queen for my own world.

[Ice and Fire: Community Edition](https://modrinth.com/mod/iceandfire-ce) ships a Dread Queen (her class, textures, name, sword and staff) but never puts her in the game. This NeoForge mod finishes her.

**Status:** in development. The Dread Queen exists as `dreadpatch:dread_queen`: spawn her with her spawn egg (Spawn Eggs tab) or `/summon dreadpatch:dread_queen`. She's a lean sovereign: 300 health behind 20 armor and 12 toughness (a full netherite set's), with a boss bar, her sword and staff, and her original model, and she rises out of the ground when spawned. She fights like a necromancer: she keeps her distance, circling at casting range, giving ground when pressed and, when cornered, breaking away with **Barrow-Step**: she steps 10 to 15 blocks away through the cold, leaving a husk of rime-glass in her shape where she stood. Whatever was hunting her turns on the husk, and when it's struck, or after 5 seconds, it shatters, rooting everyone near it for 2 seconds and touching them with Withering Rime. She saves her sword for whoever still reaches her. From range she shoots her **Withering Rime-Bolt**: a streak of her dragon's cold cyan dread fire and soot, flame-sized, that takes a share of the target's own life on top of its damage and leaves **Withering Rime**, which withers through armor, freezes and stops all healing. Her own Withering Rime feeds her: each second it withers anyone she afflicted (by her bolt, her crown's shrapnel, her sword, her Decree or her husk), she heals 1 on Easy, 2 on Normal and 3 on Hard for each of them, while she's within 32 blocks; they lose 1 a second, as under anyone's Withering Rime, and players' Withering Rime feeds no one. No share of anything's max health she takes, from any spell or her sword, is ever more than 20, so a warden or a dragon is a long fight for her too. She raises a **Rimefire Crown** round her target, a ring of ice spikes wreathed in cold cyan dread fire over cyan and black smoke, that roots everyone inside and drinks every other fire within it, then shatters it with her bolt for a burst of shrapnel. She raises her **Court of the Dread**, Ice and Fire's own thralls, ghouls, knights and scuttlers rising from the ground around her (dread scuttlers, hers and any others, now spit a weaker Withering Rime-Bolt from up to 12 blocks every 3 to 4 seconds), and with **Beater's Decree** burns one of them from behind as it presses an enemy: a burst of rime that sends the rest of her court into a frenzy. Every two minutes in a fight she calls down **Whiteout Dominion**: a blizzard 32 blocks round over the ground where she stands, lasting 45 seconds, that stays where it was raised. Inside it her enemies are slowed by a fifth, can't see past about 10 blocks through the snow-fog (mobs lose sight of anything past half their usual range), and take a point of freezing damage every 3 seconds. Snow drifts over the ground under it and melts away as it clears (it's only drawn, so it never stays behind), and from outside it shows as a wall of driven snow standing on the land. Whatever dies in her storm rises where it fell as a dread mob of her court, up to 8 a storm: a thrall in its gear from a player or anything that stands upright (a villager, a skeleton, a creeper, an enderman), a ghoul from a zombie, a scuttler from a spider, a dread horse from a horse, and a dread beast its size from an animal or anything else. Golems stay dead. Her storm clears when she falls. She drops her sword and staff, two epic or legendary inks, and one or two scrolls of her spells (Withering Rime-Bolt, Rimefire Crown, Barrow-Step, Whiteout Dominion), Ice spells (they count Ice spell power) found nowhere else. A player's Barrow-Step takes them to where they look (11 to 15 blocks, by level) and leaves their husk behind. A player's Whiteout Dominion (16 to 32 blocks round and 25 to 45 seconds, by level, on a two-minute cooldown) fogs, slows and freezes whoever they'd harm in it, but only hers raises the dead; the Dread walk through any of them untouched. Her staff is an Iron's staff now: it carries both her spells at level 3 (on your spell wheel while you hold it, cast with right-click), gives +15% Ice Spell Power and +10% Cooldown Reduction in the main hand, and through its **Permafrost Conduit** makes Ice spells cost 20% less mana while you stand on snow or ice. Her sword is unbreakable, with 11.5 attack damage at 1.6 speed and half a block more reach. Through **Thermodynamic Theft** every strike leaves 4.5 seconds of Withering Rime. Through **Glass-Rime Shatter** every third full strike on the same chilled or frozen foe (each within 5 seconds of the last; hitting others in between doesn't reset it, so it works with Better Combat's sweeping swings) shatters its frost for an extra 10% of its max health past its armor (at most 20) and leaves it **Brittle** (10% less armor) for 4 seconds. Its **Fire-Drinking Guard** rises while you hold right-click, slowing you like a shield: it stops fire and magic from the front (Iron's spells and Ice and Fire's dragon fire included; arrows and blades still get through) and puts out fire on you and around you, and a hit it stops charges the blade, which glints until its next full strike shatters at once. With the sword in your main hand, an off hand holding something with its own right-click (a shield, food, a bow, blocks, throwables, a staff) keeps it, so raise the guard with that hand empty or holding something like a totem. She swings the same sword, so her own strikes leave Withering Rime and shatter too. She doesn't spawn naturally yet. **Syndrigossa**, her undead frost wyrm, is in progress: an Ice and Fire ice dragon rotted to the bone (soot-black scales, bone showing through, torn wings), allied with the queen and her court, with a spawn egg in the Spawn Eggs tab. She breathes **Withering Rime** instead of frost, in a wide cone (20 degrees either side) rather than Ice and Fire's thin stream: it hits everyone in the cone and where it lands as hard as an ice dragon's breath of her size, leaves Withering Rime, drinks fire instead of freezing the ground, and each hit siphons half a heart more, past armor, into her. Her charge is **Shatter-Spine**: a core of bone and dread-glass that bursts 9 blocks above her target into 5 to 7 shards raining over the ground below, each cutting everyone within a block and a half and leaving Withering Rime. Every 30 seconds, once she's grown, she raises **Sovereign's Eclipse** round her target: her own Rimefire Crown, 16 blocks across with dark spires and indigo fire, standing 10 seconds. It doesn't root and doesn't close in, but everyone inside is heavily slowed (Slowness IV) and kept under Withering Rime, and it drinks the fire inside it. Her breath is big cold dread-fire flames and soot clouds you can see from the ground, Shatter-Spine flies as a burr of dark ice spikes and bursts in a ring, and her wings keep beating while she breathes on a fly-by. She takes to the air far more than other dragons (with a target on the ground she's up in about 3 seconds), and in the air she favours strafing fly-bys, breathing as she passes, and looses Shatter-Spine every 10 seconds at whatever she's hunting (she can't hunt players in creative or spectator). Tamed, she can be ridden like any Ice and Fire dragon. She grows like an ice dragon: from her spawn egg or `/summon` she's Ice and Fire's random age (1 to 80 days), with an ice dragon's health, bite and armor for it. As the boss she's ancient, at full strength (125 days: in a pack with Ice and Fire's dragon health at 1,000, that's 1,000 health, 21 bite and 20 armor): the **Ancient Syndrigossa Spawn Egg** in the Spawn Eggs tab, or `/summon dreadpatch:syndrigossa ~ ~ ~ {Ancient:1b}`, and later whatever spawns her as the boss. She's undead: Smite hits her harder, Healing hurts her and Harming heals her, and Poison, Regeneration and Wither don't take; like ice dragons she can't freeze or drown. She hunts players even when Ice and Fire's `neutralToPlayer` setting makes other wild dragons leave them alone. Her corpse is harvested like any dragon's, empty-handed, but it's Dread: her white scales or dread shards (no flesh, and no blood for a bottle), a handful of dread shards and witherbones where a dragon's heart would be, no ice dragon egg, then dragon bones and an ice dragon skull. Only the boss, an ancient Syndrigossa, also leaves her **Syndrigossa Egg** with the heart's haul, male or female. It doesn't hatch yet; her hatching ritual is still to come. Spawned as the boss, the queen rises riding an ancient Syndrigossa; until she spawns naturally, use the **Dread Queen and Syndrigossa Spawn Egg** (Spawn Eggs tab) or `/summon dreadpatch:dread_queen ~ ~ ~ {WithSyndrigossa:1b}`. In the saddle she casts her bolt and crowns while her dragon flies and fights, her court rises on the ground below her, and the two hunt the same prey. They're bound by a **Soul Tether**: while they're within 28 blocks of each other, everything her Withering Rime and the dragon's breath heal is shared between them half and half, so pull them apart and each keeps only its own. She's hard to reach in the saddle, so it's her dragon you bring down: at half its health Syndrigossa is forced to land, the queen Barrow-Steps off and fights on foot for the rest of the fight, and the dragon can't take off again for 10 seconds. (She also steps down if she falls to half her own health, or if her dragon dies under her.)

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265+, Ice and Fire CE 2.1-beta.1+ (with Uranus, Jupiter and GeckoLib, which it requires anyway), and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 3.x for 1.21.1.

## Building

`./gradlew build` puts the jar in `build/libs/`. `./gradlew runClient` starts a dev client with Ice and Fire loaded.
