# Dread Patch: notes for the next agent

NeoForge 1.21.1 mod (Java 21, ModDevGradle) that adds Ice and Fire CE's unfinished Dread Queen to the game, for the owner's modpack. Hard dependencies on Ice and Fire CE and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks; this is the owner's largest mod so far. As of 0.6.0 she's registered as `dreadpatch:dread_queen` with her original model, rises out of the ground when spawned, shoots her Withering Rime-Bolt, raises Barrow-Glass Tombs and shatters them with her bolt, and drops the loot the owner chose, scrolls of her spells included; she doesn't spawn naturally yet. Her kit's design (from the owner's notes): Withering Rime is the mark of all her magic; next comes Court of the Dread, then her staff with her two spells locked in, her sword, Whiteout Dominion, and her undead dragon Syndrigossa.

## What Ice and Fire already has (2.1-beta.1)

Read Ice and Fire's source on the `1.21.1` branch of [IAFEnvoy/IceAndFire-CE](https://github.com/IAFEnvoy/IceAndFire-CE) (`master` is the 26.1 port). That branch is ahead of the pack's 2.1-beta.1 (its `mod_version` is 2.2, unreleased), so check the jar (`~/.gradle` caches it after a build) when exact behavior matters.

- `com.iafenvoy.iceandfire.entity.DreadQueenEntity extends DreadMobEntity`: complete class. Blue boss bar, attributes in `bakeAttributes()` (max health from Ice and Fire's config `misc.dreadQueenMaxHealth`, default 750; armor 30; follow range 256), melee and target goals, a 40-tick `ANIMATION_SPAWN` (Uranus `IAnimatedEntity`), spawns holding her sword and staff, persistent.
- Ice and Fire never registers her: no `EntityType` in `IafEntities`, no attributes, spawn placement, renderer, model or spawn egg. Checked against the 2.1-beta.1 jar (`javap`, JDK at `~/.jdks/java21`): `IafEntities` registers eight dread types (`dread_beast` … `dread_thrall`, `dread_lich_skull`) and no queen.
- Textures (128×64, the same sheet size as the dread knight and lich): `iceandfire:textures/entity/dread/dread_queen.png`, `_eyes`, `_inactive`, `_injured`. Lang `entity.iceandfire.dread_queen` = "Dread Queen" in 15 languages (unused by us: our entity's key is `entity.dreadpatch.dread_queen`).
- Items, registered: `iceandfire:dread_queen_sword` ("Blade of the Dreaded Queen", tier `IafTiers.DREAD_QUEEN`) and `iceandfire:dread_queen_staff` ("Staff of the Dreaded Queen", a plain `Item` marked "no usage at the moment").
- Dragon hooks: `DragonBaseEntity` (riding tick) and `IafDragonLogic` (hovering) treat a `DreadQueenEntity` controlling passenger specially; `DragonRiderFeatureRenderer` can exclude her. The comment there ("Original logic involves riding") suggests she was meant to ride one. They test `instanceof DreadQueenEntity`, so they work with our registration.
- **Her animation never advances** in Ice and Fire: unlike `DreadLichEntity` and the other dread mobs, she never calls `AnimationHandler.INSTANCE.updateAnimations(this)`, so after `finalizeSpawn` sets `ANIMATION_SPAWN` it stays at tick 0 and never reaches a client. Our `entity/DreadQueen` makes the call. Uranus' `updateAnimations` counts the tick on both sides, sends an `AnimationPayload` (entity id + index into `getAnimations()`) to every player on the animation's first server tick, and resets to `NO_ANIMATION` at its duration; the client handler sets the animation and tick 0. The animation isn't saved, so a queen loaded from disk doesn't rise again.
- Ice and Fire tags its dread mobs `#minecraft:undead` (what makes a mob undead in 1.21.1: Smite, healing from Harming) and `#minecraft:freeze_immune_entity_types`.
- The other dread mobs render through hand-built models on `DreadBaseModel` (Uranus `HideableModelRenderer` parts), registered in `IafRenderers`. **Those can't be reused from here**: `DreadBaseModel` is package-private, and its public parent `BipedBaseModel` declares a package-private abstract `animate(...)`, so nothing outside `com.iafenvoy.iceandfire.render.model` can subclass either (and NeoForge won't load two mods sharing a package).

## What this mod adds

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `entity/DreadQueen` | Our subclass of Ice and Fire's `DreadQueenEntity`: ticks her animations (above), and while rising (`ANIMATION_SPAWN` ticks 0–29) is immobile (`isImmobile()`: no AI step, so no walking, turning or attacking) and throws block particles from the ground, like Ice and Fire's rising thralls. (The thrall instead zeroes its horizontal velocity at the end of `aiStep`, after its AI has already moved it that tick.) In `customServerAiStep` (so never while rising) she casts on her target, with line of sight: a Barrow-Glass Tomb round it every 15 s within 20 blocks (standing 5 s), her next bolt then 1.25 s later to shatter it; and her Withering Rime-Bolt every 4 s from 3-32 blocks: 8 damage + 15% of max health + 4.5 s of Withering Rime. `getEquipmentDropChance` is 0 for her hands, so her held sword and staff never drop as equipment (her loot table drops them); it overrides whatever `HandDropChances` a saved queen carries, and vanilla skips a slot whose chance is 0, so looting can't bring it back |
| `effect/WitheringRimeEffect`, `effect/DreadEvents` | **Withering Rime** (`dreadpatch:withering_rime`), the mark of pure Dread: withers (1 damage that ignores armor every 20 ticks, halved per amplifier), builds freezing 5 a tick against the body's 2-a-tick thaw, and cancels all healing (`LivingHealEvent`) while it lasts. The Dread (`IDreadMob`: her, her court, Ice and Fire's dread mobs) can't take it (`MobEffectEvent.Applicable`) |
| `magic/DreadDamage` | Game-free: the share-of-max-health part of her damage, capped at 20 against `#c:bosses` |
| `effect/BarrowGlassEffect` | **Barrow-Glass** (`dreadpatch:barrow_glass`), the root: movement speed and jump strength ×0 (`ADD_MULTIPLIED_TOTAL` −1) while it lasts |
| `entity/BarrowGlassTomb`, `magic/TombShape` | **Barrow-Glass Tomb**: a marker entity (never saved, `shouldBeSaved` false) holding a cylinder of radius 3 from 1 below its base to 2.5 above (`TombShape`, game-free). On its first tick it puts out fire blocks and lit campfires inside; every tick it renews 10 ticks of Barrow-Glass (and puts out burning) on every living thing inside it may harm (`canHarm`: not its caster, their allies, or the Dread if a Dread cast it); at its lifetime it shatters. `shatterAround`: a Withering Rime-Bolt into someone a tomb holds breaks the tombs holding them, frees them, and its shrapnel hits everyone within 3 blocks of them, them included (4 + 6% of max health, capped against bosses, + 3 s of Withering Rime; `invulnerableTime` reset so it lands right after the bolt) |
| `spell/BarrowGlassTombSpell` | `dreadpatch:barrow_glass_tomb`, Ice spell, levels 1-5, instant, 15 s cooldown: a tomb where the caster looks (Iron's `RaycastBuilder`, 24 blocks: the entity looked at, else the block, else full range; then `moveToRelativeGroundLevel`) standing 60 + 10/level ticks. Kept out of random loot and the scroll forge like the bolt |
| `client/BarrowGlassTombRenderer` | Its 9 spires: black stained glass shafts with bone-block caps drawn with `renderSingleBlock` round the ring, leaning and of varied heights; they rise over 6 ticks and sink over the last 4. The entity type is 6 wide so the ring isn't culled. Squall particles (charcoal, bone, snow) come from the entity on the client |
| `entity/WitheringRimeBolt` | Her bolt (an Iron's `AbstractMagicProjectile`): flat damage + a share of the target's max health through Iron's `DamageSources.applyDamage` with the spell's Ice damage source (so Ice resistance counts), then Withering Rime. A queen's bolt passes through the Dread. Drawn only by its trail particles (charcoal, bone, indigo dust and snowflakes; `NoopRenderer`) |
| `spell/WitheringRimeBoltSpell` | `dreadpatch:withering_rime_bolt`, an Iron's Ice spell (Ice spell power, the Cursium Mage set and the Codex of Malice count), levels 1-5, instant, 6 s cooldown: spell power (6 + 2/level) damage + 8% of max health + 4.5 s of Withering Rime. `allowLooting()` false and `setAllowCrafting(false)`: only she drops it (her loot table names it with `"force": true`) |
| `registry/ModRegistries` | `dread_queen`: an `EntityType` for our `DreadQueen` (0.6 × 1.95, the zombie's box; client tracking range 10, the Wither's); `dread_queen_spawn_egg` (`DeferredSpawnEggItem`, Ice and Fire's dread egg base `#E0E6E6` spotted with her eye glow `#00CBFF`) |
| `DreadPatch` | Her attributes (Ice and Fire's `bakeAttributes()`), the egg in the vanilla Spawn Eggs tab |
| `client/DreadQueenModel` | Ice and Fire's unused `ModelDreadQueen` (LGPL-3.0, branch 1.18.2 of AlexModGuy/Ice_and_Fire) rebuilt as a vanilla `HumanoidModel` layer: same boxes and texture offsets; extras (headware, chestplate, necklace, cloak, sleeves, robe) hang off the vanilla parts; empty `hat` because `HumanoidModel` requires one. Plays the spawn animation: Ice and Fire's `DreadBaseModel.animate` + `setRotationAnglesSpawn` redone on vanilla parts, timing in `client/RisePose` (35 px underground with arms overhead, rising over 30 ticks with Uranus' `sin(t·π/2)` ease, arms lowered over 5; the arm waving is Uranus' `flap`/`walk` formula, faded out with the arms where Ice and Fire cuts it at tick 30) |
| `client/DreadQueenRenderer` | `HumanoidMobRenderer` (held sword and staff come with it) with Ice and Fire's texture and an `EyesLayer` for `dread_queen_eyes.png`. `_inactive` and `_injured` aren't used yet |
| `data/dreadpatch/loot_table/entities/dread_queen.json` | The owner's list (0.4.0): her sword and staff, always; two inks, each epic or legendary (23 : 10, the odds of Iron's own `magic_items/great_ink`); one or two `irons_spellbooks:scroll`s of her spells: `irons_spellbooks:randomize_spell` at quality 0.5–1 with `"spell_filter": {"spells": [...], "force": true}` (force, because her spells are kept out of random loot); add each new spell of hers to that list. Quality is where in a spell's own range the level lands: level = 1 + round(quality × (maxLevel − 1)); the spell itself is picked weighted by rarity. No `killed_by_player` conditions yet; her XP is the `Monster` default |
| `tools/sprites/make_icons.py` | Draws her spell icons (16×16, full-bleed like Iron's) and effect icons (18×18 on transparency) in her palette into `src/main/resources`; deterministic, needs Pillow. Edit and re-run it rather than editing the PNGs |
| `data/.../tags/entity_type/` | `#minecraft:undead`, `#minecraft:freeze_immune_entity_types` (like Ice and Fire's dread mobs) and `#c:bosses` (other mods use it to treat her as a boss, e.g. not capturable) |

## Testing

- **Unit tests** (`./gradlew test`, part of `build`): `DreadDamageTest` pins the share-of-max-health damage and its boss cap; `TombShapeTest` the tomb's cylinder. `RisePoseTest` pins the spawn animation's curve (starts underground, rises monotonically easing out, no jumps at 30 or 35). `DreadQueenModelTest` bakes her model layer and builds the `HumanoidModel` from it. A missing or misnamed part otherwise only crashes a client while it loads, which neither the build nor a server boot reaches. Game classes are on the test classpath (`addModdingDependenciesTo(sourceSets.test)` in `build.gradle`), but there's no running game: only test code that needs none.
- **Dev server smoke test**: `tools/devserver/queen_smoke.py` (Python 3, stdlib) drives a running `./gradlew runServer` over RCON: on a stone platform at y 200: Withering Rime on an iron golem (applies, freezes, withers, blocks Instant Health) and refused by a Dread Thrall; her bolt cast through Iron's `/cast` by an armor stand (any living entity can cast with it) for 14 + 8% of a golem's 100 health and Withering Rime; the queen bolting a golem 12 blocks off; a Barrow-Glass Tomb cast by an armor stand (stands, roots: movement speed 0, puts out a fire on netherrack inside it), then shattered by a bolt (tomb gone, target freed, bolt and shrapnel ≥ 31 damage); a level 1 tomb ending by itself after 3.5 s (speed back to 0.25); the queen entombing a golem while sparing a dread thrall inside the ring. Before that, at the surface: summon, health/armor/held items, tags, 100 ticks of AI, loot (exactly one sword and one staff, two epic/legendary inks, one or two scrolls each holding one of her spells, spell levels above 1; checked on a rolled table, on a real death, and on a queen whose saved `HandDropChances` are 2.0, which dropped two of each before the `getEquipmentDropChance` override), death, the spawn egg fired from a dispenser, and the rise: with a pig 2 blocks away she must still be on her spot 25 ticks in, and off it 5 s later (without the `isImmobile` override that check failed 4 runs out of 4). Commands under `execute` need `run`; a check on a failed summon passes vacuously, so the summons are checks too; NBT tests go inside a selector's brackets (`@e[tag=x,nbt=...]`), and `has_effect` stops the run on a malformed command rather than reading it as a fail. It runs `/reload` first: Iron's Spells builds its spell config (`SpellConfigManager`, max levels etc.) on datapack sync, i.e. when a player joins or on `/reload`; on a server nobody has joined, every spell reads max level 1 and every scroll comes out level 1. Needs `enable-rcon=true`, `rcon.password=<anything>` and `server-ip=127.0.0.1` in `run/server.properties` (gitignored; set on the owner's machine already). It stops the server when done (`--keep` to leave it up).
- **Client**: rendering has no automated check. On the owner's machine `./gradlew runClient` opens a window on their desktop (there's no Xvfb), so ask before launching it. The owner's first look (0.2.0, world `run/saves/Dev_dreadpatch1`, where a queen still stands): texture right, sword and staff in hand, boss bar, attacks nearby mobs, no errors in the log. Eye glow not looked at yet.

## Working here

- **Build:** `./gradlew build`. CI (`build.yml`) runs the same on every PR and push to `main` and uploads the jar, versioned `<mod_version>-dev.<commit>` (`-dev` locally); only the Release workflow builds a plain `<mod_version>` jar.
- **Dependencies:** Ice and Fire, Uranus, Jupiter and GeckoLib come from Modrinth's maven (`maven.modrinth:<slug>:<version id>`), pinned in `gradle.properties` to the exact files the pack ships. Use version ids, not numbers: Uranus 2.4.1 names both a NeoForge and a Fabric file. They're `implementation`, so the dev runs load them as mods, along with the jars bundled inside them (Architectury from Uranus, Integration from Ice and Fire). Iron's Spells and the libraries it requires (Iron's Lib, playerAnimator, Curios) come the same way as `runtimeOnly`: only its items and loot functions are used so far; move it to `implementation` when code compiles against its API (her spells will). Optional test-only mods (JEI, Jade) go in `localRuntime`.
- **Dev runs:** `./gradlew runServer` boots to `Done` with all 13 mods (the dev environment skips the EULA check); stop it with `stop` over RCON, or SIGTERM to the java process; both save the world. Expected noise: after a `mod_version` change, NeoForge warns once that the world's `dreadpatch` version changed; Ice and Fire's `iceandfire:delight/*` recipes fail to parse (they're for Farmer's Delight, absent here), Jupiter reports missing config files on a fresh `run/`, and refmap warnings from GeckoLib, Iron's Spells, Iron's Lib and Curios. Grep the log for `FAILED during APPLY|was not applied` after changes.
- **Network:** a build needs `maven.neoforged.net`, the Mojang hosts, `api.modrinth.com` (+ `cdn.modrinth.com`), `maven.parchmentmc.org` and the foojay JDK download.
- **PRs:** into `main`. Bump `mod_version` in `gradle.properties` with user-visible changes; merging the bump releases it. Keep the README in step with behavior changes. For now (since 2026-11-14) the owner wants each PR merged as soon as its CI passes, without waiting for their review: merge commits, as they merged #1 and #2. Merge PRs that each bump the version one at a time, letting each Release run finish first: `release.yml`'s concurrency group cancels a pending run when another queues behind it, which would skip that version's release. Base PRs on `main`; a PR based on another PR's branch merges into that branch, not `main` (#2 did).
- **Release:** every push to `main` runs `release.yml`. If `v<mod_version>` isn't released yet, it builds with `RELEASE=true`, creates the tag on that commit and publishes the jar, with notes GitHub generates from the merged PRs (`.github/release-notes.md`, if present, goes above them); otherwise it stops after the check. To retry a failed release, run it by hand on `main` (GitHub MCP `actions_run_trigger`, `release.yml`, ref `main`). It never replaces an existing release. Sessions here can't push tags.
- **Shared CI:** `.github/workflows/` (`build.yml`, `release.yml`, `wiki.yml` where there's a wiki), `.github/dependabot.yml` and `.gitattributes` are identical in all the owner's mod repos (phantom_storage, golemarmor, friendlybeacons, catspells-nosumagro, gvs-noatk-players, dread-patch); phantom_storage's `CLAUDE.md` has the fullest notes. Change them in every repo at once, and keep repo-specific text out of them.
- **Modrinth:** the owner's rules: never publish anything to Modrinth (a version, the description, gallery images) without their consent, and no emoji in Modrinth text.
- **Modpack exports:** the owner drops `.mrpack` exports of their pack in the repo root for reference (gitignored). `modrinth.index.json` inside lists every mod with its Modrinth version id.
