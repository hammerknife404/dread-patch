# Dread Patch: notes for the next agent

NeoForge 1.21.1 mod (Java 21, ModDevGradle) that adds Ice and Fire CE's unfinished Dread Queen to the game, for the owner's modpack. Hard dependencies on Ice and Fire CE and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks; this is the owner's largest mod so far. As of 0.14.0 she's registered as `dreadpatch:dread_queen` with her original model, rises out of the ground when spawned, keeps her distance in a fight like Iron's necromancer (Barrow-Stepping away when cornered), shoots her Withering Rime-Bolt, raises Rimefire Crowns and shatters them with her bolt, raises her Court of the Dread and burns them for Beater's Decree, and drops the loot the owner chose, scrolls of her spells included, and her staff, which carries both her spells, and her sword, with the chat's properties; she doesn't spawn naturally yet. Her kit's design (from the owner's notes, `proposed_stuff_1.txt`, local only): Withering Rime is the mark of all her magic; still to come are Whiteout Dominion, natural spawning, and her undead dragon Syndrigossa. The owner's second notes file (`proposed_stuff_2.txt`, local only) gave Barrow-Step (0.11.0), healing from her Withering Rime and lower health (0.12.0: the owner chose 300 health, 20 armor and 12 toughness, and that only she heals), and her Rime's leech (0.13.0: a share of the victim's max health by difficulty, 1/1.5/2%, floor 1, cap 10; in the owner's world she then soloed areas full of wardens, killing one in about 10 s, so 0.14.0, the owner's choice: victims wither 1 a tick and she heals a flat 1/2/3 per target by difficulty, and every share-of-max-health hit is capped at 20 against everything). The chat's numbers for items are the other assistant's proposals; the owner chose to use them for the staff (0.9.0), and the sword (0.10.0) has the chat's profile and properties, with two gaps the owner filled: a guard-blocked hit primes a shatter (the chat's "frost charge" did nothing defined), and the guard stops only fire and magic. The shatter's 10% is ours (the chat gave none).

## What Ice and Fire already has (2.1-beta.1)

Read Ice and Fire's source on the `1.21.1` branch of [IAFEnvoy/IceAndFire-CE](https://github.com/IAFEnvoy/IceAndFire-CE) (`master` is the 26.1 port). That branch is ahead of the pack's 2.1-beta.1 (its `mod_version` is 2.2, unreleased), so check the jar (`~/.gradle` caches it after a build) when exact behavior matters.

- `com.iafenvoy.iceandfire.entity.DreadQueenEntity extends DreadMobEntity`: complete class. Blue boss bar, attributes in `bakeAttributes()` (max health from Ice and Fire's config `misc.dreadQueenMaxHealth`, default 750; armor 30; attack damage 5; speed 0.3; follow range 256; we override health, armor and toughness, below). No regeneration, melee and target goals, a 40-tick `ANIMATION_SPAWN` (Uranus `IAnimatedEntity`), spawns holding her sword and staff, persistent.
- Ice and Fire never registers her: no `EntityType` in `IafEntities`, no attributes, spawn placement, renderer, model or spawn egg. Checked against the 2.1-beta.1 jar (`javap`, JDK at `~/.jdks/java21`): `IafEntities` registers eight dread types (`dread_beast` … `dread_thrall`, `dread_lich_skull`) and no queen.
- Textures (128×64, the same sheet size as the dread knight and lich): `iceandfire:textures/entity/dread/dread_queen.png`, `_eyes`, `_inactive`, `_injured`. Lang `entity.iceandfire.dread_queen` = "Dread Queen" in 15 languages (unused by us: our entity's key is `entity.dreadpatch.dread_queen`).
- Items, registered: `iceandfire:dread_queen_sword` ("Blade of the Dreaded Queen": a plain `SwordItem`, tier `IafTiers.DREAD_QUEEN` = 4,000 uses, +4 damage, enchantability 21, repair ingredient air, built with `createAttributes(tier, 3, -2.4)`: 8 attack damage, 1.6 speed; we change it, below) and `iceandfire:dread_queen_staff` ("Staff of the Dreaded Queen", `DreadQueenStaffItem`: a bare `Item`, stack size 1, "no usage at the moment"; no recipe or tags. We make it an Iron's staff, below).
- Dragon hooks: `DragonBaseEntity` (riding tick) and `IafDragonLogic` (hovering) treat a `DreadQueenEntity` controlling passenger specially; `DragonRiderFeatureRenderer` can exclude her. The comment there ("Original logic involves riding") suggests she was meant to ride one. They test `instanceof DreadQueenEntity`, so they work with our registration.
- **Her animation never advances** in Ice and Fire: unlike `DreadLichEntity` and the other dread mobs, she never calls `AnimationHandler.INSTANCE.updateAnimations(this)`, so after `finalizeSpawn` sets `ANIMATION_SPAWN` it stays at tick 0 and never reaches a client. Our `entity/DreadQueen` makes the call. Uranus' `updateAnimations` counts the tick on both sides, sends an `AnimationPayload` (entity id + index into `getAnimations()`) to every player on the animation's first server tick, and resets to `NO_ANIMATION` at its duration; the client handler sets the animation and tick 0. The animation isn't saved, so a queen loaded from disk doesn't rise again.
- Ice and Fire tags its dread mobs `#minecraft:undead` (what makes a mob undead in 1.21.1: Smite, healing from Harming) and `#minecraft:freeze_immune_entity_types`.
- The other dread mobs render through hand-built models on `DreadBaseModel` (Uranus `HideableModelRenderer` parts), registered in `IafRenderers`. **Those can't be reused from here**: `DreadBaseModel` is package-private, and its public parent `BipedBaseModel` declares a package-private abstract `animate(...)`, so nothing outside `com.iafenvoy.iceandfire.render.model` can subclass either (and NeoForge won't load two mods sharing a package).

## Testing

- **Unit tests** (`./gradlew test`, part of `build`): `DreadDamageTest` pins the share-of-max-health damage and its cap; `CrownShapeTest` the crown's cylinder; `CourtRulesTest` her court's weights and limit; `SpacingTest` when she closes in, circles, gives ground and runs; `PermafrostConduitTest` the conduit's discount; `RimeComboTest` the sword's shatter count (per target, lapsing, the charge). `IceSpikeTest` records the crown's spike geometry through a `VertexConsumer` (block-tall segments, tapering to a point, normals outward, UVs inside the sprite). `RisePoseTest` pins the spawn animation's curve (starts underground, rises monotonically easing out, no jumps at 30 or 35). `DreadQueenModelTest` bakes her model layer and builds the `HumanoidModel` from it. A missing or misnamed part otherwise only crashes a client while it loads, which neither the build nor a server boot reaches. Game classes are on the test classpath (`addModdingDependenciesTo(sourceSets.test)` in `build.gradle`), but there's no running game: only test code that needs none.
- **Dev server smoke test**: `tools/devserver/queen_smoke.py` (Python 3, stdlib) drives a running `./gradlew runServer` over RCON: on a stone platform at y 200: Withering Rime on an iron golem (applies, freezes, withers, blocks Instant Health) and refused by a Dread Thrall; her bolt cast through Iron's `/cast` by an armor stand (any living entity can cast with it) for 14 + 8% of a golem's 100 health and Withering Rime, and into a golem given 1,000 health for 14 + 20 (the cap; uncapped, 14 + 80); the queen bolting a golem 12 blocks off, on Hard (the test sets it and puts it back), her wounded to 100 (she stays at 100 for a second, so nothing else heals her, then gains ≥ 6 over 3 s of the golem withering, 3 a tick); her crown is cleared with her, or it outlives her into the next check; a Rimefire Crown cast by an armor stand (stands, roots: movement speed 0, drinks a fire on netherrack inside it), then shattered by a bolt (crown gone, target freed, bolt and shrapnel ≥ 31 damage); a level 1 crown ending by itself after 3.5 s (speed back to 0.25); the queen crowning a golem while sparing a dread thrall inside the ring; Barrow-Step cast by an armor stand (gone from its spot, its husk there instead; the husk, struck through `/damage ... by` a golem, shatters and roots and rimes it); her sword in a husk's hand against a golem (Withering Rime on its strikes, Brittle from the third, Brittle's armor modifier; a probe of each strike's damage showed the third doing 10 more, the golem's 10%); then, on a bigger platform, her Court of the Dread against a golem under Resistance V (an invulnerable one she wouldn't target: `canBeSeenAsEnemy`): 1-3 raised, answering to her (`CommanderUUID` = her `UUID`), and Beater's Decree (a court member with Strength); ## Working here

- **Build:** `./gradlew build`. CI (`build.yml`) runs the same on every PR and push to `main` and uploads the jar, versioned `<mod_version>-dev.<commit>` (`-dev` locally); only the Release workflow builds a plain `<mod_version>` jar.
- **Dependencies:** Ice and Fire, Uranus, Jupiter and GeckoLib come from Modrinth's maven (`maven.modrinth:<slug>:<version id>`), pinned in `gradle.properties` to the exact files the pack ships. Use version ids, not numbers: Uranus 2.4.1 names both a NeoForge and a Fabric file. They're `implementation`, so the dev runs load them as mods, along with the jars bundled inside them (Architectury from Uranus, Integration from Ice and Fire). Iron's Spells and the libraries it requires (Iron's Lib, playerAnimator, Curios) come the same way, also `implementation`: her spells, bolt and crown compile against its API. Optional test-only mods (JEI, Jade) go in `localRuntime`.
- **Dev runs:** `./gradlew runServer` boots to `Done` with all 13 mods (the dev environment skips the EULA check); stop it with `stop` over RCON, or SIGTERM to the java process; both save the world. Expected noise: after a `mod_version` change, NeoForge warns once that the world's `dreadpatch` version changed; Ice and Fire's `iceandfire:delight/*` recipes fail to parse (they're for Farmer's Delight, absent here), Jupiter reports missing config files on a fresh `run/`, and refmap warnings from GeckoLib, Iron's Spells, Iron's Lib and Curios. Grep the log for `FAILED during APPLY|was not applied` after changes.
- **Network:** a build needs `maven.neoforged.net`, the Mojang hosts, `api.modrinth.com` (+ `cdn.modrinth.com`), `maven.parchmentmc.org` and the foojay JDK download.
- **PRs:** into `main`. Bump `mod_version` in `gradle.properties` with user-visible changes; merging the bump releases it. Keep the README in step with behavior changes. For now (since 2026-11-14) the owner wants each PR merged as soon as its CI passes, without waiting for their review: merge commits, as they merged #1 and #2. Merge PRs that each bump the version one at a time, letting each Release run finish first: `release.yml`'s concurrency group cancels a pending run when another queues behind it, which would skip that version's release. Base PRs on `main`; a PR based on another PR's branch merges into that branch, not `main` (#2 did).
- **Release:** every push to `main` runs `release.yml`. If `v<mod_version>` isn't released yet, it builds with `RELEASE=true`, creates the tag on that commit and publishes the jar, with notes GitHub generates from the merged PRs (`.github/release-notes.md`, if present, goes above them); otherwise it stops after the check. To retry a failed release, run it by hand on `main` (GitHub MCP `actions_run_trigger`, `release.yml`, ref `main`). It never replaces an existing release. Sessions here can't push tags.
- **Shared CI:** `.github/workflows/` (`build.yml`, `release.yml`, `wiki.yml` where there's a wiki), `.github/dependabot.yml` and `.gitattributes` are identical in all the owner's mod repos (phantom_storage, golemarmor, friendlybeacons, catspells-nosumagro, gvs-noatk-players, dread-patch); phantom_storage's `CLAUDE.md` has the fullest notes. Change them in every repo at once, and keep repo-specific text out of them.
- **Modrinth:** the owner's rules: never publish anything to Modrinth (a version, the description, gallery images) without their consent, and no emoji in Modrinth text.
- **Modpack exports:** the owner drops `.mrpack` exports of their pack in the repo root for reference (gitignored). `modrinth.index.json` inside lists every mod with its Modrinth version id.
